# Forecast FICO (2026-11-25_1545)

## FICO (Fair Isaac) Analysis as of 2026-11-25

**Snapshot:** Last close (Nov 24) was $703.33. Market cap is about $15.0B. The stock is down 57% year to date and 38% over 90 days, but it rebounded about 7.5% from the Nov 19 low of $654. The 52-week range is $586 (Sep 30, 2026) to $1,859 (Dec 12, 2025). Beta is 1.29.

### Positive Developments
1. **Mortgage distribution expansion:** The FICO Preferred Program (Classic FICO plus FICO Score 10T at a flat $10 wholesale price) was added through Xactus and XedaLink (Nov 20), Informative Research (Nov 23), and Factual Data, starting Jan 1, 2027 (Nov 25).
2. **Regulatory validation:** The FHFA published a timeline for Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac to pilot FICO Score 10T next year, which supports the modernized score's adoption.
3. **Strong fundamentals:** TTM EPS is $36.32, up 36.8% year over year. Revenue is up 27.3%, and the operating margin is about 50%.
4. **Institutional and index interest:** First Trust BuyWrite added $13.1M of FICO on Nov 20. Press coverage cites S&P 500 inclusion speculation, though this is unconfirmed.

### Potential Concerns
1. **Severe de-rating despite product wins:** The stock is down 57% YTD even as distribution deals were announced. The news does not explain the cause, which suggests the market is pricing in risks beyond these headlines.
2. **Pricing pressure and competition:** The flat $10 pricing and the FHFA's move to add a second score could erode FICO's near-monopoly on mortgage scores and compress per-score revenue.
3. **Balance sheet and valuation optics:** Total debt to equity is 8.5x and price to book is about 116x. The ROE of about 146% is inflated by a small equity base from buybacks, so it is not a clean profitability signal.
4. **High volatility:** Daily moves of 3–7% are common (e.g., -5.6% on Nov 17, +4.2% on Nov 23). The broader market also sold off on Nov 20 amid a tech selloff.

### Prediction and Reasoning
Recent news flow is net positive: several distribution wins, a regulatory timeline, and continued fund buying. The stock has also bounced off its lows with improving short-term momentum. Against that, the long-term trend is sharply negative, valuation is contested, and the volatility is very high. Next week is also a shortened trading week because of the Thanksgiving holiday on Nov 26, which reduces the number of sessions and may dampen movement. I'd expect a modest gain with wide uncertainty, so this is a low-conviction call and not investment advice.

**Data caveats:** The reported P/E of 15.3 does not reconcile with the price and TTM EPS (about 19.4x), so I relied on the EPS figures. The 3-month average volume figure appears to be in different units than the daily volumes, so I did not use it.

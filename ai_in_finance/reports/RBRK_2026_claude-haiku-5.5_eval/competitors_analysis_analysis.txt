## Year-over-Year Trends for Rubrik

Revenue grew from 600 in 2023 to 628 in 2024, a modest 4.7% increase, then accelerated sharply to 887 in 2025 (41.2% growth) and 1,316 in 2026 (48.5% growth). The 2025 and 2026 step-ups are the defining feature of the company's recent history, as the revenue base more than doubled over the period.

Gross margin moved from 69.7% in 2023 to 76.9% in 2024, dipped to 70.0% in 2025, and then rose to 80.1% in 2026. The 2025 decline is the one notable reversal in an otherwise improving trend, and the 2026 figure is the highest in the series.

EBITDA margin was deeply negative throughout. It was -39.2% in 2023 and -43.5% in 2024, then fell to -121.6% in 2025 when EBITDA reached -1,078 on revenue of 887. In 2026 the margin improved markedly to -20.7% as EBITDA narrowed to -272. The 2025 figure is clearly an outlier relative to the rest of the series, and the resource does not explain its cause.

Free cash flow turned from negative to positive: -15 in 2023, -25 in 2024, 22 in 2025, and 238 in 2026. The 2026 figure represents roughly 18% of revenue, a clear inflection point.

ROIC was 21.1% in 2023 and 19.5% in 2024, spiked to 356.1% in 2025, and then fell to -30.4% in 2026. The 2025 value is not plausible as a steady-state measure and likely reflects a distortion in the invested capital base or in earnings, which the resource does not explain. The 2026 negative figure is consistent with the company remaining loss-making at the operating level.

FCF conversion and EV/EBITDA are labeled n/m (not meaningful) for every year, so no trend can be drawn from them. The only valuation multiple available for Rubrik is EV/Sales, which was 15.85 in 2025 and 9.18 in 2026, showing a substantial compression as revenue grew faster than enterprise value.

## Metric-Specific Insights

**EBITDA Margin.** Rubrik's profitability lags its peers in the most recent year. Its -20.7% margin compares with positive margins of 9.0% at Commvault and 14.4% at Nutanix, and only SentinelOne's -25.4% is lower. The direction of travel is favorable, since Rubrik improved by roughly 100 percentage points from its 2025 trough, but it remains the only company in the group with a margin that is both negative and well short of break-even at scale in 2026.

**EV/EBITDA.** Rubrik's EV/EBITDA is not meaningful in any year, so the resource provides no direct valuation comparison on this metric. Among peers with available figures, Commvault and Nutanix traded at 30 to 75 times EBITDA, and the premium of Nutanix in 2024 (166.21) is an extreme outlier. Because Rubrik's EBITDA is negative, its enterprise value cannot be benchmarked against earnings, and the more relevant indicator is EV/Sales, which shows Rubrik at a premium to every peer in 2026.

**FCF Conversion.** Rubrik's FCF conversion is n/m in every year, so cash flow efficiency cannot be measured on the conversion basis. In absolute terms, however, Rubrik's free cash flow turned positive in 2025 and reached 238 in 2026, which compares with Commvault's 237 in 2026 and Nutanix's 841. Commvault's conversion ratios of 1.18, 2.68, and 3.36 across 2024 to 2026 show consistently strong cash generation relative to EBITDA, and Nutanix's 3.98 in 2025 is similarly high, while its 0.56 in 2026 signals a sharp drop. Rubrik's cash flow inflection is real but has not yet been expressed as a conversion ratio.

**Gross Margin.** Rubrik's gross margin expanded from 69.7% in 2023 to 80.1% in 2026, a gain of about 10 percentage points, which indicates improving cost efficiency in delivering its subscriptions. Its 2025 dip to 70.0% is the notable exception. Across the period it remained below Commvault and Nutanix, which held margins in the 81% to 87% range, but it moved ahead of SentinelOne in 2024 and 2025 and has converged with Commvault by 2026.

**ROIC.** Rubrik's ROIC was positive and competitive in 2023 and 2024, at 21.1% and 19.5%, ahead of most peers. The 2025 value of 356.1% is an anomaly that the resource does not explain and should not be interpreted as sustained efficiency. The 2026 value of -30.4% signals that the company still generates negative returns on the capital employed, which contrasts with Commvault and Nutanix, both positive at about 8% to 10% in 2026. The recent trend therefore points to a deterioration in return efficiency even as operations improved.

**Revenue and Revenue Growth.** Rubrik's revenue more than doubled from 600 in 2023 to 1,316 in 2026, the fastest growth trajectory among the four companies in the most recent two years. The acceleration from 4.7% in 2024 to 41.2% in 2025 and 48.5% in 2026 is the most striking pattern in the dataset. Its absolute revenue remains smaller than Nutanix's 2,854 and modestly above Commvault's 1,184 in 2026, but Rubrik has grown past SentinelOne's 1,001 and Commvault's 2026 level. The growth rate is far above Nutanix's 12.4% in 2026, which signals that Rubrik is taking share in a fast-growing category.

## Conclusion

Rubrik's financial profile across these metrics is one of rapid top-line expansion combined with a gradual improvement toward operational breakeven and positive free cash flow. Revenue growth is the strongest feature, with growth rates above all three competitors in 2025 and 2026, and gross margin has climbed to 80.1%, nearly matching Commvault. Free cash flow has moved from -25 in 2024 to 238 in 2026, which is a meaningful change in the cash generation profile. Weaknesses remain substantial: EBITDA margin is still -20.7% against positive margins at two peers, ROIC turned negative in 2026, and the 2025 values for EBITDA margin and ROIC are extreme outliers that complicate interpretation.

The valuation picture is that Rubrik trades at a sizeable premium on revenue, with an EV/Sales of 9.18 in 2026 compared with 5.86 for Nutanix, 4.56 for SentinelOne, and 2.73 for Commvault. Because its EBITDA is negative, the EV/EBITDA metric cannot justify the price, and Rubrik's multiple is therefore supported only by expectations for future growth and margin expansion rather than by current earnings. The historical data partially supports that expectation: the margin trajectory is clearly improving, and growth is accelerating, so the premium is not unreasonable on these metrics alone. However, the negative ROIC in 2026 and the persistent operating losses mean that current financial performance does not on its own justify the valuation, and the premium depends on sustained execution to convert revenue growth into positive EBITDA and returns on capital.

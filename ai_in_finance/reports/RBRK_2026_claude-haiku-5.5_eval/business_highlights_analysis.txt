**Rubrik Security Cloud (RSC)**

RSC is Rubrik's core business and has represented a majority of total revenue since the end of fiscal 2024, with Subscription ARR reaching $1.46 billion (up 34%) and Cloud ARR reaching $1.29 billion (up 48%) as of January 31, 2026. Subscription revenue rose 53% to $1.26 billion, supported by ARR growth and roughly $70.2 million of non-recurring material-rights revenue, while the average subscription dollar-based net retention rate stayed above 120% and customers with $100,000 or more in Subscription ARR increased to 2,805 from 2,246.

**Rubrik Agent Cloud (RAC)**

RAC is the newest suite, launched to provide an AI operations platform for monitoring, controlling, and remediating agentic actions, and it became commercially available only in February 2026, after fiscal 2026 ended. The filing provides no separate financial results for RAC, and management expects RSC to remain the majority of revenue and the main driver of ARR growth in fiscal 2027.

**Other Revenue (legacy perpetual licenses, Rubrik-branded Appliances, and professional services)**

Other revenue declined 10% to $52.3 million and fell to 4% of total revenue from 7% a year earlier. The decrease reflects the largely completed migration of maintenance customers to subscription editions and the transition of appliance sales to contract manufacturers, and management expects this share to keep declining, with professional services becoming the main driver over time.

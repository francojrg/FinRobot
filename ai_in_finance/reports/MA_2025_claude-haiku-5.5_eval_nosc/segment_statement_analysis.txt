**Note on scope:** The resources do not report separate segment profit, market share, or named strategic partners. The MD&A splits net revenue into two components, which I analyze below. Company net income rose 16% to $14,968 million in 2025 (2024: $12,874 million), while the effective tax rate rose to 19.4% from 15.6%, primarily due to the Singapore net tax effect and the Pillar 2 Rules.

**Payment Network**

Net revenue rose 12% to $19,476 million in 2025 from $17,335 million, driven by 9% GDV growth and 18% cross-border volume growth (USD basis). Rebates and incentives to customers reached $20,522 million, up 16%, which offsets gross volume gains. Segment-level costs and profit are not disclosed.

**Value-Added Services and Solutions**

Net revenue rose 23% to $13,315 million from $10,832 million; acquisitions contributed 3 points and currency-neutral growth was 21%. Growth came from security, digital and authentication, consumer acquisition, pricing and business insights. Personnel costs for strategic initiatives drove G&A higher, and capitalized software amortization lifted D&A 27%. Segment profit is not disclosed.

**Company Overview and Industry**

Incorporated in Delaware in July 2004 (the filing gives an incorporation date, not a founding date), Meta Platforms, Inc. is a social technology company that provides Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, Threads, WhatsApp, and Meta AI, plus Reality Labs hardware such as Meta Quest headsets and Ray-Ban Meta AI glasses. Advertising generated $196.18 billion of the $200.97 billion total 2025 revenue (about 98%, illustrative).

**Core Strengths and Key Products**

Family daily active people reached 3.58 billion on average in December 2025, up 7%. Ad impressions rose 12% and average price per ad rose 9% in 2025. The Family of Apps (FoA) operating margin was 52%, down from 54%, as costs grew faster than revenue.

**Market Position, End Markets, and Customers**

Revenue grew 21% in United States & Canada, 24% in Europe, 20% in Asia-Pacific, and 27% in Rest of World. Impression growth is concentrated in Asia-Pacific, which monetizes at lower rates. Marketers buy through a self-service platform, agencies, and resellers. The filing does not name major customers, disclose customer concentration, or state a market share figure.

**Trends, Opportunities, and Challenges**

Regulation and platform changes limit ad targeting and measurement, including the EU consent model and Apple's iOS changes. Reels monetizes at a lower rate than Feed and Stories. Reality Labs lost $19.19 billion from operations in 2025 and is expected to lose a similar amount in 2026. Free cash flow fell 16% to $43.59 billion, as property and equipment purchases rose to $69.69 billion. Capital expenditures are guided to $115–135 billion in 2026. Other revenue rose 50% to $2.58 billion, driven by WhatsApp paid messaging and Meta Verified.

**Strategic Initiatives and Response**

In 2025 the company launched Meta Ray-Ban Display with the Meta Neural Band, following the 2024 Orion AR prototype. It is investing in AI across the discovery engine and ad tools, and it trains a mix of open and closed models. Capital returns totaled $31.57 billion, including $26.26 billion of buybacks, and the quarterly dividend rose to $0.525.

---

**Summary**

Meta's 2025 revenue grew 22% to $200.97 billion, and operating income grew 20% to $83.28 billion. Net income fell 3% to $60.46 billion because of a $15.93 billion One Big Beautiful Bill Act tax charge. Free cash flow fell 16% as AI infrastructure spending rose, and Reality Labs lost $19.19 billion. Advertising is nearly all revenue, and regulation limits targeting. The thesis would be challenged if ad price growth slows below impression growth, or if 2026 capital spending exceeds the $115–135 billion guidance without matching revenue growth.

**Business segments:** Family of Apps (FoA), which includes Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp, and other services, and Reality Labs (RL), which covers virtual and augmented reality hardware, software, and content.

**Consolidated context:** Net income fell 3% to $60.46 billion in 2025 from $62.36 billion in 2024. The tax provision rose to $25.47 billion from $8.30 billion, which includes a $15.93 billion OBBBA charge recorded in the third quarter.

### Family of Apps

Family of Apps (FoA) revenue rose 22% to $198.76 billion from $162.36 billion, driven by impressions (+12%) and price per ad (+9%). Operating income rose 18% to $102.47 billion, but margin fell to 52% from 54% as costs rose 28% to $96.29 billion on compensation and infrastructure. EU consent changes and Apple iOS limits constrain targeting.

### Reality Labs

Reality Labs (RL) revenue rose 3% to $2.21 billion from $2.15 billion, led by AI glasses, partly offset by lower Meta Quest sales. The operating loss widened 8% to $19.19 billion from $17.73 billion as costs rose 8% to $21.40 billion, mainly on compensation and inventory commitment losses. Management expects 2026 losses similar to 2025.

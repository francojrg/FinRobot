Operating cash flow rose 27% to $115.80 billion in 2025, but free cash flow fell 16% to $43.59 billion from $52.10 billion, because capital expenditures, including finance lease principal, nearly doubled to $72.22 billion from $39.23 billion. Investing outflows reached $102.00 billion, including $18.33 billion of non-marketable equity purchases. Operating cash was helped by cash taxes of $7.58 billion, down from $10.55 billion. Financing was a $20.37 billion net outflow: $29.91 billion of net note proceeds was outweighed by $26.26 billion of buybacks, $18.40 billion of RSU tax payments and $5.32 billion of dividends. Cash and securities rose $3.78 billion to $81.59 billion. Against 2026 capex guidance of $115–135 billion, holding operating cash flow at the 2025 level would leave a shortfall of about $9 billion at the midpoint (illustrative, before financing).

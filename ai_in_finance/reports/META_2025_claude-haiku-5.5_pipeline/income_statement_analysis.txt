Revenue rose 22% to $200.97 billion, but net income fell 3% to $60.46 billion as a $15.93 billion tax charge lifted the effective tax rate to 30%.

**Revenue Drivers**
Advertising delivered $196.18 billion (+22%) on 12% more ad impressions and a 9% higher average price per ad. Other revenue rose 50% to $2.58 billion.

**Cost Pressure**
Cost of revenue grew 20% but held at 18% of revenue. R&D rose 31% to $57.37 billion, the largest cost increase, driven by compensation and AI infrastructure.

**Margins**
Gross margin edged up to 82.0% from 81.7%. Operating margin slipped to 41% from 42%, and net margin fell to 30% from 38%.

**Earnings Per Share**
Diluted EPS fell 2% to $23.49, partly offset by a 1.5% lower diluted share count.

**Reality Labs Risk**
Reality Labs lost $19.19 billion from operations in 2025, and management expects 2026 losses to remain similar.

Meta's balance sheet grew faster than its liquidity in 2025. Total assets rose 33% to $366.02 billion, and net property and equipment rose 44% to $196.80 billion, or 53.8% of assets, up from 49.4%. Total debt rose 71% to $83.90 billion from $49.06 billion, and long-term debt more than doubled to $58.74 billion from $28.83 billion. Liquidity remains adequate: current assets of $108.72 billion cover current liabilities of $41.84 billion at 2.60x, down from 2.98x. Total liabilities to equity rose from 0.51x to 0.69x. Equity of $217.24 billion grew 19%, led by retained earnings of $121.18 billion. Reported net debt is $22.87 billion. The main risk sits off the balance sheet: $103.77 billion of leases not yet commenced and $131.05 billion of contractual commitments.

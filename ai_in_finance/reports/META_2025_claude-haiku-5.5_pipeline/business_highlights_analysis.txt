**Family of Apps (FoA):** FoA revenue rose 22% to $198.76 billion in 2025 from $162.36 billion in 2024, and segment income from operations rose 18% to $102.47 billion, but the operating margin fell to 52% from 54%. Advertising drove the gain: ad impressions grew 12% and average price per ad grew 9%, while FoA costs and expenses rose 28% to $96.29 billion, mainly from employee compensation, infrastructure, partner arrangements, and legal-related costs.

**Reality Labs (RL):** RL revenue grew only 3% to $2.21 billion in 2025 from $2.15 billion, and the segment loss from operations widened 8% to $19.19 billion from $17.73 billion, leaving an operating margin of (870)% against (826)% a year earlier. Revenue growth came from higher sales of AI glasses, which was partly offset by lower Meta Quest sales, while RL costs and expenses rose 8% to $21.40 billion on higher employee compensation, estimated losses on non-cancelable purchase commitments for RL inventory, and technology development costs, and management expects 2026 RL operating losses to remain similar to 2025.

Operating cash flow rose to $139.5 billion in 2025 from $115.9 billion, but free cash flow fell 71% to $11.2 billion from $38.2 billion. Cash capital expenditures reached $128.3 billion, up from $77.7 billion, mainly for AWS technology infrastructure. Investing outflows totaled $142.5 billion, up from $94.3 billion. Financing turned to a $9.7 billion inflow, with $25.0 billion of new debt against $15.3 billion of repayments, and no revolver or commercial paper borrowings were outstanding at year-end. Cash taxes fell to $8.3 billion from $12.3 billion under the 2025 Tax Act, flattering operating cash flow. Cash and marketable securities of $123.0 billion give adequate liquidity. The view would be wrong if free cash flow stays near 2025 levels once the tax benefit fades.

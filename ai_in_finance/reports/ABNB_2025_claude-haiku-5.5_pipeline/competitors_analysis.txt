**Conclusion**

ABNB's financial profile across these metrics is solid but not superior. Revenue has grown every year, reaching 12,241 in 2025. Gross margin has held above 82% and FCF has grown to 4,646. These are real strengths. The weaknesses are equally clear: revenue growth has decelerated for two consecutive years, EBITDA was flat in 2025, the EBITDA margin has moved within a 15% to 24% range with no clear upward trend, and ROIC in 2025 is the lowest in the peer set.

The current EV/EBITDA of 30.35 is about 1.8 times the peer average. The data supports a premium to peers in some respects, such as cash generation and gross margin relative to MAR, but it does not support a premium of this size against BKNG, which leads on growth, margin, and returns. As an illustration, if ABNB's EV stayed at roughly 77,200, a multiple equal to BKNG's 15.78 would imply EBITDA of about 4,900, roughly 92% above the 2025 figure. The valuation therefore depends on EBITDA growing substantially from current levels.

The view would be proven wrong if the 2025 pattern persisted, with revenue growth below 10% and EBITDA margin below 20% in 2026, and ROIC stuck near 20%. Conversely, a return of EBITDA growth to or above revenue growth, with margin recovering toward the 23% level of 2022 and 2024, would support the current multiple more strongly. The resources do not include forward estimates, so these tests can only be checked against future reported figures.

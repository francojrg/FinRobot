# Rocket Lab Corporation (RKLB): Financial Metrics Compared with Firefly Aerospace Inc. (FLY), Intuitive Machines, Inc. (LUNR), and Planet Labs PBC (PL)

The resource does not state units for the monetary figures, so values are discussed as presented. Fiscal year ends may differ across the four companies, so year labels are compared as given. Several metrics are reported as n/a or n/m for many company-years, and those gaps are noted where they limit the analysis.

## Revenue and Revenue Growth

Rocket Lab's revenue rose from 211 in 2022 to 245 in 2023, 436 in 2024, and 602 in 2025. Growth was 15.9% in 2023, accelerated sharply to 78.3% in 2024, and moderated to 38.0% in 2025. The 2022 growth figure is n/a. The trajectory is one of sustained expansion, with the 2024 step-up being the most significant change in the series. Growth decelerated in 2025 but the revenue base still grew by roughly 166 units over the two years.

Against peers, Rocket Lab was the largest revenue generator in every year where all companies are reported. In 2022 it stood at 211 against Intuitive Machines' 86. In 2023 it reported 245 against Planet Labs' 191, Intuitive Machines' 80, and Firefly's 55. In 2024 it reported 436 against Intuitive Machines' 228, Planet Labs' 221, and Firefly's 61. In 2025 it reported 602 against Planet Labs' 244, Intuitive Machines' 207, and Firefly's 160.

Growth comparisons are more mixed. Firefly grew 10.1% in 2024 but then 163.0% in 2025, a figure that exceeds Rocket Lab's 38.0% for the same year, though from a much smaller base. Intuitive Machines posted -7.4% in 2023, a 186.6% surge in 2024, and -9.2% in 2025, which shows that its revenue is highly volatile. Planet Labs grew 15.4% in 2024, 10.7% in 2025, and 25.9% in the 2026 column. Rocket Lab's 2025 growth was therefore well above Planet Labs and well above Intuitive Machines' decline, but below Firefly's 2025 jump.

## Gross Profit and Gross Margin

Rocket Lab's gross margin improved in every year presented, from 9.0% in 2022 to 21.0% in 2023, 26.6% in 2024, and 34.4% in 2025. Gross profit rose from 19 to 207 over the same period. This is one of the clearest improvement trends in the dataset and suggests that the company is gaining cost efficiency as its revenue scales.

Relative to peers, Rocket Lab's margin was below Firefly's 48.2% and Planet Labs' 49.2% in 2023, though well above Intuitive Machines' -30.7%. In 2024, Rocket Lab's 26.6% was above Intuitive Machines' 1.2% and Firefly's -18.7%, but below Planet Labs' 51.2%. In 2025, Rocket Lab's 34.4% exceeded Firefly's 19.2% and Intuitive Machines' 2.9%, but trailed Planet Labs' 57.2%. Planet Labs is the clear margin leader in the group and has maintained margins above 49% throughout, with its 2026 figure at 56.1%. Intuitive Machines' gross margins were near zero or negative, indicating weak pricing or cost control on its contracts during this period.

## EBITDA and EBITDA Margin

Rocket Lab's EBITDA losses were -95 in 2022, -132 in 2023, -130 in 2024, and -155 in 2025. The absolute loss therefore widened over the period, even as revenue grew. The EBITDA margin, however, improved markedly from -45.1% in 2022 and -53.8% in 2023 to -29.7% in 2024 and -25.8% in 2025. This pattern indicates that revenue growth outpaced the growth in operating losses, so the company is narrowing its losses relative to sales.

In 2025, Rocket Lab's -25.8% margin was the least negative of the four companies. Planet Labs posted -30.4%, Intuitive Machines -45.9%, and Firefly -181.9%. Rocket Lab's margin trend in 2023 and 2024 was also stronger than Firefly's, which ran at -224.3% in 2023 and -321.8% in 2024. Intuitive Machines showed very large swings, from -5.3% in 2022 to -73.9% in 2023 and -151.3% in 2024, before improving to -45.9% in 2025. Planet Labs' margins moved from -69.2% in 2023 to -41.7% in 2024 and -30.4% in 2025 before the 2026 column showed -64.0% with EBITDA of -197. Rocket Lab's 2025 profitability position is therefore the strongest in the peer set on this measure, though the company remains loss-making at the EBITDA level.

## EV/EBITDA

EV/EBITDA is reported as n/m for all four companies in every year, so the resource does not provide a basis for a direct EV/EBITDA valuation comparison. This is consistent with negative EBITDA across the group. The question of whether Rocket Lab is over- or undervalued on this metric therefore cannot be answered from the resource.

The resource does provide EV/Sales and Price/Book figures that serve as partial substitutes. Rocket Lab's EV/Sales rose from 8.07 in 2022 to 11.11 in 2023, 29.91 in 2024, and 67.38 in 2025. In each year, this is the highest multiple in the group. Intuitive Machines traded at 2.1, 1.17, 7.27, and 8.49 over the same years, Planet Labs at 6.2 in 2023, 2.7 in 2024, 7.1 in 2025, and 27.97 in 2026, and Firefly at 19.26 in 2025. Rocket Lab's 2025 EV/Sales of 67.38 is more than three times Firefly's 19.26 and roughly eight times Planet Labs' 7.1. The market is therefore paying a substantial premium per dollar of revenue, a premium that has expanded steadily as the company's revenue has grown. Rocket Lab's Price/Book ratio followed a similar pattern, rising from 2.66 in 2022 and 4.88 in 2023 to 33.59 in 2024 before easing to 23.88 in 2025, compared with Firefly's 2.99 in 2025 and Planet Labs' 4.15 in 2025.

## FCF and FCF Conversion

Rocket Lab's free cash flow was -149 in 2022, -154 in 2023, -116 in 2024, and -322 in 2025. The 2024 improvement was reversed sharply in 2025, when the cash burn more than doubled. FCF conversion is n/m for Rocket Lab in every year because EBITDA is negative, so the company's cash generation relative to earnings cannot be assessed through this ratio. As a derived check, the 2025 free cash outflow equals roughly 53% of revenue.

Among peers, Firefly's free cash burn was -171, -190, and -238 across 2023 to 2025, and Intuitive Machines' was -16, -75, -68, and -56 across 2022 to 2025. Planet Labs' burn was -87, -93, and -69 for 2023 to 2025, then turned to +51 in the 2026 column, the only positive free cash flow in the group. Rocket Lab's 2025 burn of -322 was the largest in the dataset in absolute terms. The only FCF conversion figure reported for any peer is Intuitive Machines' -1.22 in 2023, and the remaining peer values are n/m. Rocket Lab therefore lags on cash efficiency in the most recent year, and the widening burn is the most significant negative trend in its profile.

## Return on Invested Capital

Rocket Lab's ROIC was -12.0% in 2022, -18.2% in 2023, -15.5% in 2024, and -9.3% in 2025. The company's returns were negative throughout, but the trend has improved since 2023, with the 2025 figure the least negative in the series. This suggests that the company's capital is being deployed more productively relative to its operating results, although it still does not earn a positive return.

Peer ROIC figures vary widely. Intuitive Machines posted positive returns of 14.8%, 23.6%, 25.6%, and 17.5% across 2022 to 2025. Firefly posted 31.5% in 2023 and 33.6% in 2024 before falling to -18.9% in 2025. Planet Labs' returns were -22.6%, -20.1%, -20.2%, and -22.5% across 2023 to 2026. In 2025, Rocket Lab's -9.3% ranked second in the group behind Intuitive Machines' 17.5%, and ahead of Firefly's -18.9% and Planet Labs' -20.2%. Firefly's ROIC reversal in 2025 is notable and may reflect its expanded capital base or losses, but the resource does not explain the cause.

## Conclusion

Rocket Lab's financial profile shows a company that is scaling quickly and improving its operating economics. Revenue grew from 211 to 602 over four years and was the largest in the peer group in each shared year. Gross margin rose every year to 34.4%, and the EBITDA margin improved from -53.8% in 2023 to -25.8% in 2025, which was the least negative in the group in the most recent year. ROIC has also moved from -18.2% to -9.3%. These trends support the view that operational execution is improving.

Several weaknesses remain. The absolute EBITDA loss grew to -155 in 2025, free cash burn more than doubled to -322, and the company still has negative returns on capital and negative EBITDA, so none of the EBITDA-based or cash-based valuation measures can be used to justify the price. Its valuation multiples are the highest in the group by a wide margin, with EV/Sales at 67.38 in 2025 compared with peers ranging from 7.1 to 19.26, and Price/Book at 23.88. The resource contains no market capitalization, forward estimates, or EV/EBITDA values, so it cannot establish whether the market price is justified. Based on the reported figures, the premium appears to rest on expectations of future revenue growth and margin expansion that are not yet visible in cash flow or earnings. The reported trends support those expectations to a degree, particularly in gross margin and EBITDA margin, but the rising cash burn and negative ROIC mean the valuation depends heavily on the company converting its growth into positive cash generation over time.

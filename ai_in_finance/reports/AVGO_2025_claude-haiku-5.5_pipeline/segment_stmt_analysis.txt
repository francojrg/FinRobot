**Semiconductor Solutions**

Semiconductor solutions revenue rose 22% to $36,858 million from $30,096 million (+$6,762 million), led by networking demand, mainly custom AI accelerators and AI networking products. Operating income rose 27% to $21,232 million from $16,759 million; illustrative operating margin was 58% versus 56%. No market share figure is disclosed. One distributor was 32% of net revenue, up from 28%.

**Infrastructure Software**

Infrastructure software revenue rose 26% to $27,029 million from $21,478 million (+$5,551 million), driven by VCF demand, license revenue on contracts where customers cannot terminate, and the subscription transition. Operating income rose 49% to $20,765 million from $13,977 million (+$6,788 million), with illustrative margin of 77% versus 65%, helped by lower labor costs after the VMware integration.

Operating cash flow rose to $27,537 million in fiscal 2025 from $19,962 million, but working capital absorbed $8,500 million, up from $4,637 million, mainly through a $2,717 million receivables build after a $2,327 million release a year earlier. Stock-based compensation of $7,568 million, a non-cash add-back, also supports the operating figure. Investing outflow fell to $580 million from $23,070 million because the prior year included $25,416 million paid for VMware. Financing outflow reached $20,127 million: $11,142 million of dividends, $18,478 million of debt repayments partly offset by $15,666 million of new borrowings, and $6,310 million of share repurchases and withholding taxes. Cash ended at $16,178 million. Capital expenditures of $623 million are expected to rise in fiscal 2026, when management also expects a material global minimum tax effect on cash flows.

**Semiconductor solutions:** Segment net revenue rose 22% to $36,858 million in fiscal 2025 from $30,096 million in fiscal 2024, and segment operating income rose 27% to $21,232 million from $16,759 million. The increase came from strong demand for the company's networking solutions, primarily custom AI accelerators and AI networking products.

**Infrastructure software:** Segment net revenue rose 26% to $27,029 million from $21,478 million, and segment operating income rose 49% to $20,765 million from $13,977 million. Growth was driven mainly by demand for the VCF product, including license revenue recognized on contracts where customers do not have the right to terminate, and by the shift to a subscription license model, while lower labor costs after the VMware integration added to the margin gain.

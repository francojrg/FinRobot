Rubrik, Inc. (RBRK) has the fastest-growing top line in this peer set, but its profitability and return metrics remain the weakest or most volatile. The analysis below covers fiscal years 2023 through 2026, using the year labels exactly as they appear in the data. The resource notes that fiscal year ends differ across these companies, and the figures have not been adjusted for that difference, so year-by-year comparisons should be read with that in mind. Several metrics also contain internal inconsistencies, which I flag where they matter.

**Revenue and Revenue Growth**

Rubrik's revenue rose from 600 in 2023 to 628 in 2024, then to 887 in 2025 and 1,316 in 2026. Growth was nearly flat at 4.7% in 2024, then accelerated sharply to 41.2% in 2025 and 48.5% in 2026. The 2024 slowdown followed by a sharp reacceleration is the defining pattern of the company's recent history, and the 2026 revenue base is more than double the 2023 level.

In 2023, Rubrik's revenue of 600 was the smallest in the group, well below Commvault's 785, Nutanix's 1,863, and SentinelOne's 422 would be the only lower figure. Actually, SentinelOne's 422 was lower than Rubrik's 600, so Rubrik ranked third of four that year, ahead of only SentinelOne. In 2024, Rubrik's 628 still trailed Commvault (839), Nutanix (2,149), and SentinelOne (621 was lower, so Rubrik was marginally ahead of SentinelOne on revenue that year). Rubrik's growth of 4.7% in 2024 was the lowest in the group, while Commvault grew 7.0%, Nutanix 15.3%, and SentinelOne 47.1%.

In 2025, Rubrik's revenue of 887 passed SentinelOne's 821 and moved into third place, still behind Commvault (996) and Nutanix (2,538). Its growth of 41.2% was the second-highest in the group, behind SentinelOne's 32.2%? No, it was higher: 41.2% exceeded SentinelOne's 32.2%, Nutanix's 18.1%, and Commvault's 18.6%, making Rubrik the fastest grower that year. In 2026, Rubrik's revenue of 1,316 surpassed Commvault's 1,184 and moved into third place by revenue, still behind Nutanix's 2,854 and ahead of SentinelOne's 1,001. Its 48.5% growth was the highest in the group, ahead of SentinelOne's 21.9%, Commvault's 18.9%, and Nutanix's 12.4%.

**Gross Margin**

Rubrik's gross margin moved from 69.7% in 2023 to 76.9% in 2024, fell back to 70.0% in 2025, and then rose to 80.1% in 2026. The 2025 dip coincides with the year of fastest revenue growth, which suggests that the cost base scaled faster than revenue during that period, though the data does not explain the cause. The 2026 reading is the highest Rubrik has recorded in the table.

Comparing across peers, Rubrik was the lowest in 2023 at 69.7%, below SentinelOne (65.8% was lower, so Rubrik was above SentinelOne), Commvault (82.7%), and Nutanix (82.2%). Rubrik was therefore second-lowest that year, ahead only of SentinelOne. In 2024, Rubrik's 76.9% was above SentinelOne's 71.1% but well below Commvault's 81.9% and Nutanix's 84.9%. In 2025, Rubrik's 70.0% was the lowest in the group, below SentinelOne's 74.3%, Commvault's 82.0%, and Nutanix's 86.8%. In 2026, Rubrik's 80.1% moved to second place, behind Nutanix's 86.8% but ahead of Commvault's 81.2%? No, it was slightly below Commvault's 81.2%, so Rubrik ranked third, ahead of SentinelOne's 74.1%. Nutanix led throughout the period, and Commvault held a stable margin in the low-to-low-80s range.

**EBITDA Margin**

Rubrik's EBITDA margin was deeply negative in every year: -39.2% in 2023, -43.5% in 2024, -121.6% in 2025, and -20.7% in 2026. The 2025 figure, driven by EBITDA of -1,078 on revenue of 887, is the most severe reading in the table. The 2026 margin of -20.7% represents a major improvement, with EBITDA of -272 on revenue of 1,316, and it is the least negative figure Rubrik has recorded in this period.

Relative to peers, Rubrik was the least profitable company in every year except that SentinelOne was also negative in every year, with margins of -83.6%, -53.6%, -34.9%, and -25.4%. Rubrik was therefore worse than SentinelOne in 2023 and 2024 and in 2025, but in 2026 Rubrik's -20.7% was slightly better than SentinelOne's -25.4%. Commvault moved from -0.6% in 2023 to 10.8% in 2024, 9.1% in 2025, and 9.0% in 2026, placing it in positive territory from 2024 onward. Nutanix moved from -5.0% in 2023 to 3.8%, 11.5%, and 14.4%, reaching the highest margin in the group by 2026. The most recent year therefore shows Rubrik as the second-weakest on EBITDA margin, ahead only of SentinelOne, while Nutanix and Commvault are profitable on this measure.

**FCF Conversion and Free Cash Flow**

Rubrik's free cash flow was -15 in 2023, -25 in 2024, 22 in 2025, and 238 in 2026. The turn to positive free cash flow in 2025 and the strong figure in 2026 are meaningful, because the business generated more than 200 in free cash flow in its most recent year despite a negative EBITDA. FCF conversion, however, is reported as "n/m" for Rubrik in every year, which is appropriate given the negative EBITDA base, so the table does not allow a conversion-based comparison for this company.

Commvault's free cash flow was 167, 200, 204, and 237 across the four years, a steady and predictable pattern. Its reported FCF conversion was 1.18 in 2024, 2.68 in 2025, and 3.36 in 2026. Nutanix's free cash flow rose from 207 in 2023 to 598, 750, and 841, with reported conversion of 3.98 in 2025 and 0.56 in 2026. SentinelOne's free cash flow was -212 in 2023, -87 in 2024, 7 in 2025, and 52 in 2026, so it moved from heavy cash burn toward modest positive generation, but conversion is "n/m" in all years.

The conversion figures themselves do not reconcile with the EBITDA and FCF values in the same table. For example, Commvault's 2024 FCF of 200 on EBITDA of 90 implies a ratio of about 2.2, not the reported 1.18, and Nutanix's 2026 figure implies about 2.05 rather than 0.56. The conversion metric's definition is not given in the resource, so I cannot determine which basis it uses. The reported ratios should therefore be used with caution, and the free cash flow levels themselves offer the more reliable comparison. On that basis, Rubrik's 2026 free cash flow of 238 is comparable to Commvault's 237 and well below Nutanix's 841, while SentinelOne's 52 is far lower.

**ROIC**

Rubrik's ROIC was 21.1% in 2023, 19.5% in 2024, 356.1% in 2025, and -30.4% in 2026. The 2025 reading is an extreme outlier, and it cannot be interpreted as a sustained return on capital, particularly because the same year showed EBITDA of -1,078. The table does not explain the figure, and it may reflect a very small or unusual invested capital base, but that cannot be confirmed from the data. The 2026 figure of -30.4% is strongly negative, which is consistent with the company's ongoing operating losses at the EBITDA level, even though free cash flow turned positive that year. Taken together, the ROIC series for Rubrik is volatile and does not show a clear improving trend.

In 2023, Rubrik's 21.1% was the highest in the group, while Commvault (-5.9%), Nutanix (-24.6%), and SentinelOne (-22.7%) were negative. In 2024, Rubrik's 19.5% remained positive and was below Commvault's 22.5% but above Nutanix's -3.6% and SentinelOne's -17.3%. In 2025, Rubrik's reported 356.1% was far above Commvault's 23.5% and Nutanix's 30.2%, but as noted, this figure is not a reliable guide to underlying efficiency. In 2026, Rubrik's -30.4% was the weakest in the group, below SentinelOne's -12.9%, Nutanix's 9.9%, and Commvault's 8.3%. The most recent year therefore shows Rubrik lagging its peers on this measure, with returns on invested capital that have deteriorated sharply.

**EV/EBITDA and Valuation**

Rubrik's EV/EBITDA is reported as "n/m" in every year, because its EBITDA was negative throughout. This means the multiple cannot be used to judge whether Rubrik is over or undervalued against peers on earnings. Commvault's EV/EBITDA was 45.51 in 2024, 73.63 in 2025, and 30.4 in 2026, while Nutanix's was 166.21 in 2024, 71.53 in 2025, and 40.81 in 2026. SentinelOne's EV/EBITDA is also "n/m" in all years because of its negative EBITDA. In 2024, Commvault and Nutanix were the only two companies with a meaningful EV/EBITDA, and Nutanix's multiple was far higher. In 2025 and 2026, both companies' multiples compressed, with Commvault at 30.4 and Nutanix at 40.81 by 2026, though both remained well above what a mature, slower-growing profile might suggest.

Because EBITDA-based comparisons are unavailable for Rubrik and SentinelOne, EV/Sales offers the most useful cross-company valuation context. Rubrik's EV/Sales was 15.85 in 2025 and 9.18 in 2026, the highest in the group in both years. SentinelOne's EV/Sales was 9.99 in 2023, 12.77 in 2024, 9.29 in 2025, and 4.56 in 2026. Nutanix's ranged from 4.32 in 2023 to 8.25 in 2025 and 5.86 in 2026, and Commvault's from 2.84 in 2023 to 6.7 in 2025 and 2.73 in 2026. In 2023, Rubrik's EV/Sales was not available, but SentinelOne's 9.99 and Nutanix's 4.32 and Commvault's 2.84 show that the market has long assigned premium revenue multiples to high-growth security and data companies. By 2026, Rubrik's 9.18 remained roughly double Nutanix's 5.86 and more than three times Commvault's 2.73, which indicates that the market is pricing Rubrik as a high-growth asset well ahead of its more profitable peers.

**Conclusion**

Rubrik's financial profile is one of strong and accelerating top-line growth combined with a still-unproven earnings base. Revenue more than doubled between 2023 and 2026, and growth reached 48.5% in the most recent year, the highest in the group. Gross margin recovered to 80.1% in 2026, and free cash flow reached 238, a level comparable to Commvault's. Those are meaningful improvements. Against that, EBITDA margin remained negative at -20.7% in 2026, ROIC turned sharply negative at -30.4%, and the 2025 ROIC and EBITDA readings were so extreme that they make the trend difficult to assess.

The current valuation appears to rest mainly on growth rather than on present earnings. With EV/EBITDA unavailable, the premium EV/Sales multiple of 9.18 in 2026 is the clearest signal, and it is about three times the level of Commvault and roughly 1.6 times the level of Nutanix. That premium is partly supported by Rubrik's 48.5% growth, which exceeds every peer, and by its improving margin and cash flow trajectory. However, the same metrics do not yet demonstrate that the business earns returns on its capital or converts its scale into EBITDA profitability on the level of Nutanix or Commvault. The valuation therefore depends on continued growth and on the company reaching sustainable profitability, and the financial data in this resource supports the growth component of that case more strongly than the profitability component.

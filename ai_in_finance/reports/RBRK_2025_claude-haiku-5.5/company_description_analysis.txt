**Company Overview and Industry**

Founded in a year not disclosed in the provided resources, Rubrik, Inc. is a cybersecurity company that provides Zero Trust Data Security through Rubrik Security Cloud (RSC), a cloud-native SaaS platform that secures data across enterprise, cloud, and SaaS applications. Its products cover data protection, data threat analytics, data security posture, and cyber recovery. Rubrik operates in the data security market, where it competes with data management vendors and specialized security providers.

**Core Strengths and Competitive Advantages**

Rubrik combines backup, recovery, and cybersecurity on a single architecture that uses time-series data and metadata. Its Zero Trust design features native immutability, a logical air gap, and multi-factor authentication. Its AI and machine learning threat engine analyzes self-describing data. Subscription ARR reached $1,092.6 million, up 39%, and Cloud ARR reached $875.6 million, up 67%. Average subscription dollar-based net retention exceeded 120%. Customers with $100,000 or more in Subscription ARR rose to 2,246.

**Market Position**

Rubrik sells through a global sales team and a two-tier indirect Channel Partner model. International revenue from EMEA and Asia-Pacific grew from $186.4 million to $250.4 million. The resources do not disclose market share or name specific major customers, although they describe customers ranging from the largest global organizations to mid-sized companies across many industries.

**Industry Trends, Opportunities, and Challenges**

Rising cyberattack volumes, generative AI adoption, data privacy regulation, and hybrid multi-cloud and SaaS adoption create demand for data-layer security. Challenges include intensifying competition from Commvault, Veeam, and Cohesity, revenue fluctuations from the subscription transition through fiscal 2027, rising hosting costs, and reliance on Supermicro for appliance manufacturing.

**Recent Strategic Initiatives**

Rubrik completed its IPO in April 2024. It acquired Laminar, a data security posture management platform, in August 2023. It launched Ruby, a generative AI data defense product, and has shifted customers from legacy CDM to RSC subscriptions. It also maintains a co-engineering partnership with Microsoft.

---

**Summary**

Rubrik is a Zero Trust data security provider whose subscription model is growing rapidly, with Subscription ARR up 39% and Cloud ARR up 67%. Its differentiated architecture and high net retention support its position. However, the company posted a net loss of $1,154.8 million, largely from $913.9 million in stock-based compensation after its IPO. Free cash flow was $21.6 million. Revenue will remain volatile through fiscal 2027 as customers transition, and competition is intensifying. Its investment in AI and international expansion position it for growth, but profitability remains a key watch item.

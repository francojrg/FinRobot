This analysis therefore uses the three revenue categories in the income statement and MD&A: Subscription, Maintenance, and Other. Net profit is reported only on a consolidated basis: a net loss of $1,154.8 million (loss per share $7.48) versus $354.2 million in fiscal 2024, driven by operating expenses of $1,754.8 million versus $789.4 million, including $913.9 million of stock-based compensation. The filing does not provide segment-level profit, quantified market share, named strategic partnerships, or effects from national policy changes.

**Subscription**

Subscription revenue rose 54% to $828.7 million from $537.9 million, the core growth driver. Subscription ARR grew 39% to $1,092.6 million, and Cloud ARR grew 67% to $875.6 million. Gross profit rose 39% to $613.7 million, but margin fell to 74% from 82%, due to $49.5 million of IPO-related stock-based compensation and a $48.5 million increase in hosting costs.

**Maintenance**

Maintenance revenue fell 52% to $18.4 million from $38.7 million, shrinking to 3% of total revenue from 6%. Gross profit dropped 62% to $12.3 million, and margin declined to 67% from 83%, mainly from IPO-related stock-based compensation. Management expects the transition of maintenance customers to RSC to be largely complete by the end of fiscal 2026.

**Other**

Other revenue, mainly Rubrik-branded Appliance sales and professional services, declined 23% to $39.4 million, as appliance sales fell $10.4 million during the transition to contract manufacturers. Gross profit swung to a $5.2 million loss (margin of -13% versus 21%), as cost of other revenue rose 10% to $44.6 million, including $14.5 million of stock-based compensation.

Rubrik's fiscal 2025 operating cash flow turned positive at $48.2 million, compared with $(4.5) million in fiscal 2024 and $19.3 million in fiscal 2023. Free cash flow was $21.6 million, after a $22.8 million payroll tax outlay tied to IPO-related equity vesting. Investing outflows of $383.4 million were driven mainly by net investment purchases, with capital spending of $26.6 million. Financing inflows of $398.0 million reflected $815.2 million of IPO proceeds, partly offset by $432.5 million of taxes paid on net share settlement of vested awards. Cash rose $189.3 million to $392.7 million. With $705.1 million in cash and short-term investments against $322.3 million of debt due in fiscal 2029, management says liquidity is sufficient for at least 12 months. Cash generation remains thin relative to the $1,154.8 million net loss.

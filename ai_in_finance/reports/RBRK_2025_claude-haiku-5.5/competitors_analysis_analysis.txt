# Financial Comparison: Rubrik (RBRK) Against Commvault (CVLT), Nutanix (NTNX), and Zscaler (ZS)

The analysis uses the fiscal year labels exactly as they appear in the data (2023 through 2026). Fiscal year ends differ across these companies, so each labeled year is a comparison of reported periods rather than calendar-aligned periods. Two data caveats apply throughout. The FCF Conversion figures for Commvault and Nutanix do not reconcile to a simple ratio of the FCF and EBITDA values shown in the same table, and the resource does not state the calculation basis, so those figures are discussed as reported. The Rubrik ROIC figure of 356.1% in 2025 is an outlier that the resource does not explain.

## Revenue

Rubrik's revenue rose from 600 in 2023 to 628 in 2024, 887 in 2025, and 1,316 in 2026. The 2024 increase was marginal, but the 2025 and 2026 increases were substantial, and revenue more than doubled over the two years from 2024 to 2026. Rubrik began the period as the smallest company in the group. Its 2026 revenue of 1,316 exceeded Commvault's 1,184, moving Rubrik from fourth to third place among the four companies. Rubrik still trails Zscaler (3,353) and Nutanix (2,854) by a wide margin. In 2023, Nutanix led with 1,863, followed by Zscaler at 1,617, Commvault at 785, and Rubrik at 600. In 2024, Commvault and Rubrik had both lagged the two larger peers, and Rubrik's 628 remained the smallest figure. Commvault's revenue grew more steadily, moving from 785 to 839, 996, and 1,184, which gives it a consistent but slower trajectory than Rubrik's recent acceleration.

## Revenue Growth

Rubrik's revenue growth accelerated from 4.7% in 2024 to 41.2% in 2025 and 48.5% in 2026. This is the most pronounced acceleration in the group. In 2024, Rubrik was the slowest grower, behind Commvault (7.0%), Nutanix (15.3%), and Zscaler (34.1%). In 2025, Rubrik grew fastest, ahead of Zscaler (23.3%), Commvault (18.6%), and Nutanix (18.1%). The 2026 figures show the same ranking at the top, with Rubrik at 48.5%, Zscaler at 25.4%, Commvault at 18.9%, and Nutanix at 12.4%. Nutanix's growth decelerated in 2026 and was the slowest in the group that year, while Zscaler's growth has been stable in the mid-20s. Commvault's growth has been steady in the high teens for two years. Rubrik's trajectory is therefore the strongest in the group in the most recent two years, though the 2023 base is not available for growth calculation, and the company's growth is measured from a small starting point.

## Gross Margin

Rubrik's gross margin was 69.7% in 2023, rose to 76.9% in 2024, fell back to 70.0% in 2025, and rose again to 80.1% in 2026. Rubrik had the lowest gross margin in the group in 2023, 2024, and 2025. In 2023, Commvault (82.7%) and Nutanix (82.2%) were well ahead, and Zscaler (77.6%) was also above Rubrik. In 2024, Nutanix (84.9%), Commvault (81.9%), and Zscaler (78.0%) all exceeded Rubrik's 76.9%. In 2025, Rubrik's 70.0% was the lowest in the group, with Zscaler at 76.9%, Commvault at 82.0%, and Nutanix at 86.8%. The 2026 figure of 80.1% moves Rubrik ahead of Zscaler (76.8%) but still below Commvault (81.2%) and Nutanix (86.8%). The swing from 70.0% to 80.1% in a single year is the largest movement in the group, and the resource does not explain its source. Nutanix's gross margin has risen steadily from 82.2% to 86.8%, while Commvault's and Zscaler's margins have drifted slightly lower over the period. Rubrik's gross margin has the most volatility, which makes it harder to assess the company's underlying cost efficiency from these figures alone.

## EBITDA Margin

Rubrik's EBITDA margin was -39.2% in 2023, -43.5% in 2024, -121.6% in 2025, and -20.7% in 2026. The 2025 figure reflects an EBITDA loss of 1,078 on revenue of 887, and the 2026 figure reflects a loss of 272 on revenue of 1,316. Rubrik's margin improved substantially in 2026, but it remains deeply negative. Rubrik ranked last among the four companies in every year.

The peer group presents a clear profitability gradient. Commvault was the only company with a margin close to break-even in 2023 at -0.6%, then moved to 10.8% in 2024, 9.1% in 2025, and 9.0% in 2026. Nutanix moved from -5.0% in 2023 to 3.8%, 11.5%, and 14.4% in 2026, and is the most improved peer. Zscaler moved from -6.8% in 2023 to 2.8%, 4.0%, and 5.3% in 2026, which shows steady but modest improvement. In the most recent year, Nutanix led at 14.4%, followed by Commvault at 9.0%, Zscaler at 5.3%, and Rubrik at -20.7%. Rubrik's 2026 margin is roughly 26 percentage points below Zscaler, the lowest-margin profitable peer, which indicates that Rubrik remains in a much earlier stage of profitability than the other three companies.

## EV/EBITDA

Rubrik's EV/EBITDA is not meaningful in any year because its EBITDA has been negative throughout the period. This means that the standard earnings-based valuation test cannot be applied to Rubrik, and the comparison must rely on other measures.

For the peers, Commvault's EV/EBITDA was 45.51 in 2024, rose to 73.63 in 2025, and fell to 30.4 in 2026. Nutanix's EV/EBITDA was 166.21 in 2024, 71.53 in 2025, and 40.81 in 2026. Zscaler's was 445.89 in 2024, 412.62 in 2025, and 143.31 in 2026. Zscaler carried the highest earnings multiple in every year, which reflects both the high valuation the market assigns to its business and its comparatively small EBITDA base. Nutanix's multiple fell sharply over the period as its EBITDA grew, and Commvault's multiple is the lowest of the three in 2024 and 2026. In 2025, Nutanix (71.53) and Commvault (73.63) were nearly identical, and both were far below Zscaler.

Because Rubrik's EV/EBITDA cannot be computed, the EV/Sales ratio provides the most useful available comparison. Rubrik's EV/Sales was 15.85 in 2025 and 9.18 in 2026, with no data for 2023 or 2024. In 2025, Rubrik's multiple was second only to Zscaler's 16.69 and well above Nutanix (8.25) and Commvault (6.7). In 2026, Rubrik's 9.18 was the highest in the group, above Zscaler (7.63), Nutanix (5.86), and Commvault (2.73). Rubrik's EV/Sales fell by roughly 6.7 turns between 2025 and 2026, even as its revenue grew 48.5%, so the market has been paying less per dollar of revenue. Even so, the company still carries the richest sales multiple among the four companies in the most recent year. Taken together, the peer comparison indicates that Rubrik is valued on expected growth rather than current earnings, and the available data cannot establish whether that valuation is excessive or reasonable. Among the peers, Commvault appears least richly valued on both measures in 2026.

## FCF Conversion

Rubrik's FCF conversion is not meaningful in any year, and Zscaler's is also not meaningful in any year, even though Zscaler's EBITDA and free cash flow are both positive in the later years. The resource does not explain why these two companies' figures are marked as not meaningful, so no inference should be drawn about their underlying cash conversion from this metric.

Commvault's FCF conversion was not meaningful in 2023 but rose to 1.18 in 2024, 2.68 in 2025, and 3.36 in 2026. Nutanix's conversion was not meaningful in 2023 and 2024, rose to 3.98 in 2025, and then fell to 0.56 in 2026. Commvault's conversion trend indicates that its free cash flow has grown faster than its EBITDA in recent years, which is consistent with its FCF rising steadily from 167 to 237. Nutanix's sharp drop in 2026 reflects a decline in conversion even as its free cash flow rose from 750 to 841. Because Nutanix's 2025 and 2026 conversion figures move in opposite directions, its conversion trend cannot be interpreted cleanly from this metric alone.

The raw free cash flow figures give more context than the conversion ratios. Rubrik's free cash flow moved from -15 in 2023 to -25 in 2024, then turned positive at 22 in 2025 and reached 238 in 2026. That is a meaningful improvement and the first sustained evidence of cash generation in the group's data for Rubrik. Commvault generated positive free cash flow in all four years, growing from 167 to 237. Nutanix's free cash flow rose from 207 to 841, and Zscaler's rose from 334 to 779. Rubrik's 2026 free cash flow of 238 is therefore comparable to Commvault's, but it remains far below Nutanix's and Zscaler's.

## ROIC

Rubrik's ROIC was 21.1% in 2023 and 19.5% in 2024, both of which compared favorably with the peers in 2023 and were close to Commvault's 22.5% in 2024. The 2025 figure of 356.1% is an extreme outlier that is far above every peer, and the resource does not explain it, so it should not be taken as evidence of efficient capital deployment. The 2026 figure of -30.4% is the lowest ROIC in the group and represents a sharp deterioration.

The peers present a different pattern. Commvault's ROIC moved from -5.9% in 2023 to 22.5% in 2024, 23.5% in 2025, and then fell to 8.3% in 2026. Nutanix's ROIC moved from -24.6% in 2023 to -3.6% in 2024, 30.2% in 2025, and 9.9% in 2026, showing a strong recovery followed by a moderate decline. Zscaler's ROIC has been negative in every year, moving from -7.5% in 2023 to -0.7%, -0.3%, and -0.2%, which indicates that its returns have approached zero but have not turned positive. In 2026, Commvault (8.3%) and Nutanix (9.9%) were both positive, Zscaler was marginally negative at -0.2%, and Rubrik was the weakest at -30.4%. The recent trend in Rubrik's returns is therefore a clear and significant reversal, and the 2026 figure suggests that the company's current invested capital is not yet generating returns.

## Conclusion

Rubrik's financial profile across these metrics is one of rapid top-line expansion and recent cash flow improvement, set against persistent losses and a sharp deterioration in returns. Its revenue growth is the strongest in the group in both 2025 and 2026, and its revenue has moved from the smallest in the peer set to ahead of Commvault. Its gross margin recovered to 80.1% in 2026, and its free cash flow turned positive and rose to 238. These are genuine positives. However, its EBITDA margin remains at -20.7% in 2026, the lowest in the group by a wide margin. Its ROIC fell to -30.4% in 2026, the weakest figure among the four companies, after a 2025 value that appears to be an outlier. Its valuation also cannot be assessed on an earnings basis because EV/EBITDA is not meaningful in any year.

Whether the current valuation is justified depends on which measures the investor weights. The 48.5% revenue growth rate and the improvement in free cash flow and gross margin provide a basis for a growth-oriented valuation, and Rubrik's EV/Sales of 9.18 in 2026 is the highest in the group, which the market has presumably assigned in anticipation of continued expansion. The negative EBITDA margin and deeply negative ROIC in 2026, however, offer little evidence from current profitability or returns that the business is yet generating value for shareholders on the capital it employs. Commvault demonstrates what a mature, consistently profitable profile looks like in this group, with stable margins of roughly 9% to 11% and positive ROIC in the most recent years, and it carries a lower sales multiple of 2.73. Nutanix shows the most complete improvement in profitability and cash generation, with its EBITDA margin rising to 14.4% and its EV/Sales at 5.86. Rubrik's valuation is therefore best understood as a bet on sustained growth and a future margin inflection, and the data in this resource supports the growth and cash flow improvement but does not yet demonstrate the profitability or return on capital that would confirm that bet.

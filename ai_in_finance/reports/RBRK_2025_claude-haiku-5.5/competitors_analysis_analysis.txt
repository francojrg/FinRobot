# Rubrik, Inc. (RBRK): Financial Metrics Analysis Against Commvault Systems, Inc., Varonis Systems, Inc., and SentinelOne, Inc.

Rubrik, Inc. (RBRK) is compared here with Commvault Systems, Inc. (CVLT), Varonis Systems, Inc. (VRNS), and SentinelOne, Inc. (S). The fiscal years are labeled as given in the data, and the comparison covers 2023 through 2026 for Rubrik. Varonis data ends in 2025 and carries a 2022 observation that Rubrik lacks. The resource does not state the currency or scale of the dollar figures, so revenue, EBITDA, and FCF are discussed in the units presented. Several metrics are marked "n/m" (not meaningful) for most companies, which limits direct comparison on EV/EBITDA and FCF conversion.

## Revenue and Revenue Growth

Rubrik's revenue rose from 600 in 2023 to 628 in 2024, then to 887 in 2025 and 1,316 in 2026. Growth was only 4.7% in 2024, which marked a sharp slowdown relative to the years that followed. It then accelerated to 41.2% in 2025 and 48.5% in 2026. Revenue more than doubled over the period, and the trajectory is steeply upward, with the largest absolute gain in 2026.

Against its peers, Rubrik's 2024 growth of 4.7% was the lowest in the group. Commvault grew 7.0%, Varonis 10.4%, and SentinelOne 47.1% that year. In 2025, Rubrik's 41.2% growth was the second-highest, trailing only SentinelOne's 32.2% for that year only in the sense that SentinelOne was in fact lower, so Rubrik led the group, ahead of SentinelOne (32.2%), Varonis (13.2%), and Commvault (18.6%). In 2026, Rubrik's 48.5% was again the highest, versus SentinelOne at 21.9% and Commvault at 18.9%. Varonis had no 2026 data. In terms of scale, Rubrik's 2026 revenue of 1,316 exceeded Commvault's 1,184 and SentinelOne's 1,001 in that year.

SentinelOne's growth profile is a useful contrast. Its growth decelerated each year from 47.1% to 32.2% to 21.9%, while Rubrik's accelerated. Commvault and Varonis showed steadier, more moderate expansion, with Varonis's growth rising from 5.4% in 2023 to 10.4% in 2024 and 13.2% in 2025. Rubrik's combination of scale and acceleration is the most distinctive revenue profile in the peer set, though the data alone does not explain its drivers.

## Gross Margin

Rubrik's gross margin moved from 69.7% in 2023 to 76.9% in 2024, fell to 70.0% in 2025, and then rose to 80.1% in 2026. The 2025 dip coincides with the year of heaviest revenue growth, and the 2026 rebound to the highest level in its series is a significant improvement. The resource does not explain the cause of the swings, so the pattern should be treated as observed rather than explained.

In 2023, Rubrik's 69.7% trailed Commvault at 82.7%, Varonis at 85.3% for 2022 (the nearest comparable year available for Varonis), and SentinelOne at 65.8%. Rubrik was ahead only of SentinelOne. In 2024, Rubrik's 76.9% stayed below Commvault's 81.9% and Varonis's 85.6%, but it was above SentinelOne's 71.1%. In 2025, Rubrik's 70.0% lagged Commvault (82.0%), Varonis (78.8%), and SentinelOne (74.3%), making it the lowest in the group that year. In 2026, Rubrik's 80.1% was the highest of any company in the data for that year, ahead of Commvault (81.2%) and SentinelOne (74.1%).

Commvault's gross margin was the most stable in the group, staying between 81.2% and 82.7% across all four years. Varonis's margin declined steadily from 85.6% in 2023 to 83.0% in 2024 and 78.8% in 2025, which is a notable erosion for a company with historically high margins. SentinelOne improved from 65.8% to 74.3% before flattening at 74.1% in 2026, suggesting its cost structure matured. Rubrik's margin is the most volatile in the set.

## EBITDA Margin

Rubrik's EBITDA margin was -39.2% in 2023, -43.5% in 2024, -121.6% in 2025, and -20.7% in 2026. EBITDA was -235, -273, -1,078, and -272 in those years. The 2025 figure is a pronounced outlier, and the margin improved markedly in 2026 to the least negative level in Rubrik's series. The 2026 improvement is real within the data, but Rubrik remained loss-making at the EBITDA line.

Rubrik's margins were the weakest in the group in 2023 and 2024. Commvault was at -0.6% in 2023 and then moved positive, reaching 10.8% in 2024, 9.1% in 2025, and 9.0% in 2026. Varonis was at -20.2% in 2022, -14.4% in 2023, -12.2% in 2024, and -15.6% in 2025. SentinelOne was at -83.6% in 2023, -53.6% in 2024, -34.9% in 2025, and -25.4% in 2026. In 2023, Rubrik's -39.2% was better than SentinelOne's -83.6% but worse than Varonis's -14.4% and Commvault's -0.6%. In 2024, Rubrik's -43.5% trailed Commvault's 10.8%, Varonis's -12.2%, and SentinelOne's -53.6% was actually worse. In 2025, Rubrik's -121.6% was far below all peers. In 2026, Rubrik's -20.7% was better than SentinelOne's -25.4% but well below Commvault's 9.0%, the only peer with positive EBITDA margins across the recent years.

SentinelOne showed the most consistent improvement in EBITDA margin among the loss-making companies, closing much of its gap over the period. Commvault demonstrated that profitability at the EBITDA level was achievable in this peer group from 2024 onward.

## FCF Conversion

FCF conversion is "n/m" for Rubrik in every year because its EBITDA was negative, so a ratio is not meaningful. The underlying free cash flow, however, moved from -15 in 2023 to -25 in 2024, then turned positive at 22 in 2025 and 238 in 2026. The 2026 figure is large relative to the -272 EBITDA, which means cash generation diverged sharply from reported EBITDA in that year. That gap is worth investigating before drawing conclusions about underlying cash efficiency, since the resource does not explain it.

Commvault is the only peer with a reported FCF conversion figure: 1.18 in 2024, 2.68 in 2025, and 3.36 in 2026. These numbers indicate that free cash flow exceeded EBITDA in each year, and the ratio rose steadily. However, the conversion values do not reconcile with the FCF and EBITDA figures in the same table. Commvault's FCF of 200 divided by its EBITDA of 90 gives about 2.2 for 2024, not 1.18, and the 2025 and 2026 figures similarly differ from the stated ratios. Commvault's free cash flow itself was steady at 167, 200, 204, and 237 across 2023 through 2026. Given this discrepancy, the reported conversion metric should be verified against the underlying definition before being relied upon.

Varonis and SentinelOne also show "n/m" for FCF conversion in every year. Varonis's FCF rose from 0 in 2022 to 54, 102, and 132 in 2023, 2024, and 2025, a steady improvement. SentinelOne's FCF improved from -212 in 2023 to -87 in 2024, 7 in 2025, and 52 in 2026, crossing into positive territory in 2025. Rubrik's move to positive FCF in 2025 and its strong 2026 figure place it on a similar trajectory to SentinelOne, though at a much larger scale in the 2026 numbers.

## Return on Invested Capital

Rubrik's ROIC was 21.1% in 2023 and 19.5% in 2024, both respectable levels that were slightly declining. The 2025 figure of 356.1% is an extreme outlier that does not fit the rest of the series or the company's negative EBITDA in that year, and the resource offers no explanation for it. The 2026 figure of -30.4% reverses sharply. Taken as reported, the series is too volatile to support a clear conclusion about Rubrik's capital efficiency, and the 2025 and 2026 values should be verified before use.

Relative to peers, Rubrik's 2023 and 2024 ROIC were well above those of the others. Commvault was at -5.9% in 2023 before rising to 22.5% in 2024 and 23.5% in 2025, then falling to 8.3% in 2026. Varonis was negative throughout, between -10.6% and -5.0%. SentinelOne was also negative throughout, improving gradually from -22.7% to -12.9%. In 2025, Rubrik's 356.1% far exceeded Commvault's 23.5%, but that comparison is distorted by the outlier. In 2026, Rubrik's -30.4% was the weakest in the group, below SentinelOne's -12.9% and well behind Commvault's 8.3%.

Commvault's ROIC trajectory is the cleanest evidence of value creation in this set, having moved from negative to a sustained positive range before easing in 2026. Varonis and SentinelOne consistently earned negative returns on invested capital, which is consistent with their losses over the period. The 2026 decline in Commvault's ROIC, despite growing EBITDA and FCF, is worth noting as well.

## Enterprise Value Multiples and Valuation

Rubrik's EV/EBITDA is "n/m" in every year because EBITDA was negative, so the standard earnings-based multiple cannot be used to judge whether the stock is over- or undervalued. The EV/Sales multiple is the only available valuation anchor. It was 15.85 in 2025 and fell to 9.18 in 2026. Rubrik's revenue growth of 48.5% in 2026 is consistent with that compression, since faster revenue expansion against a relatively stable enterprise value would lower the multiple. Even so, 9.18 times sales is high relative to the peers.

Commvault's EV/EBITDA was 45.51 in 2024, 73.63 in 2025, and 30.4 in 2026, which is the only earnings multiple available in the peer group. Those figures show a rich valuation in 2024 and 2025, with a substantial compression in 2026 as earnings grew. Varonis and SentinelOne have "n/m" EV/EBITDA throughout, so no direct earnings-based comparison is possible with them. On EV/Sales, Rubrik's 2025 multiple of 15.85 was far above Commvault (6.7), Varonis (6.7), and SentinelOne (9.29). In 2026, Rubrik's 9.18 was above SentinelOne's 4.56 and Commvault's 2.73. Across 2023 and 2024, Rubrik has no EV/Sales figure, while Commvault traded at 2.84 and 4.9, Varonis at 10.06 and 10.11, and SentinelOne at 9.99 and 12.77.

Taken together, Rubrik has consistently carried a premium revenue multiple relative to most peers in the years for which it is reported. That premium is difficult to justify on profitability alone, since its EBITDA margins remain deeply negative. Its premium is more defensible on growth, given the 41.2% and 48.5% revenue expansion in 2025 and 2026, which exceeds every peer in those years.

## Conclusion

Rubrik's financial profile across these metrics is one of rapid top-line expansion, improving gross margins in 2026, a turn to positive free cash flow, and a clear narrowing of EBITDA losses in 2026, offset by continued negative EBITDA and volatile ROIC figures that require verification. Revenue growth is the strongest feature, accelerating to 48.5% in 2026 and placing Rubrik at the top of the peer group on that measure in both 2025 and 2026. Its gross margin reached 80.1% in 2026, the highest in the group that year, which suggests improving unit economics as the business scales. Free cash flow reached 238 in 2026, a result that stands in contrast to the negative EBITDA of -272 and warrants scrutiny.

The weaker aspects are profitability and return measures. EBITDA margin was -20.7% in 2026, after the severe -121.6% in 2025, and it remained the least favorable in the group apart from SentinelOne in several years. The ROIC series is unreliable as reported, with an extreme 356.1% in 2025 followed by -30.4% in 2026, and neither figure is explained by the data.

The valuation is harder to justify on current earnings. With EV/EBITDA not meaningful for Rubrik, the market is effectively pricing the company on revenue. A 9.18 EV/Sales multiple in 2026 is well above Commvault's 2.73 and SentinelOne's 4.56, and it is supported mainly by the growth rate rather than by earnings or returns. Commvault's EV/EBITDA of 30.4 in 2026 shows that the market pays a premium even for a profitable peer, so Rubrik's premium is consistent with a growth-led thesis. Whether that thesis is justified depends on whether the high growth converts into sustained EBITDA and ROIC improvement. The current metrics show encouraging movement in that direction, but not yet the level of profitability that would validate the valuation on conventional earnings measures.

**Subscription:** Subscription revenue rose 54% to $828.7 million in fiscal 2025, and gross profit grew 39% to $613.7 million. Growth was driven by a 39% increase in Subscription ARR to $1,092.6 million, along with modest benefits from customers exercising or forfeiting Subscription Credits, but subscription gross margin fell from 82% to 74% because of $49.5 million in post-IPO stock-based compensation and $48.5 million in higher hosting costs.

**Maintenance:** Maintenance revenue fell 52% to $18.4 million, and gross profit declined 62% to $12.3 million. The decline reflects the shrinking perpetual license base as existing customers move to RSC subscriptions, a transition expected to be largely complete by the end of fiscal 2026, while gross margin dropped from 83% to 67% due to $3.1 million of stock-based compensation recognized after the IPO.

**Other:** Other revenue, which consists mainly of Rubrik-branded Appliance sales and professional services, decreased 23% to $39.4 million, and gross profit swung to a $5.2 million loss from a $10.7 million profit. The decline stems mainly from a $10.4 million drop in appliance sales as the transition to contract manufacturers is largely complete, while the negative gross margin of (13)% was driven by $14.5 million in post-IPO stock-based compensation within cost of other revenue.

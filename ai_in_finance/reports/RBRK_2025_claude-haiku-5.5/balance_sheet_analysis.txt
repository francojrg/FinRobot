For the fiscal year ended January 31, 2025, Rubrik's liquidity improved markedly. Current assets of $1,077.6 million covered current liabilities of $950.2 million (current ratio 1.13, up from 0.84), and working capital swung to $127.5 million from a $107.6 million deficit. Total assets reached $1,423.0 million against $1,976.7 million of liabilities, leaving a stockholders' deficit of $553.7 million, much narrower than the $1,419.3 million deficit a year earlier. Long-term debt rose to $322.3 million, about 23% of assets. Deferred revenue of $1,419.5 million, largely customer prepayments, dominates the liability base. Cash and short-term investments grew to $705.1 million from $279.3 million, enhancing flexibility. Leverage is moderate, but the thin equity base means long-term prospects depend on sustained subscription growth converting deferred revenue into cash.

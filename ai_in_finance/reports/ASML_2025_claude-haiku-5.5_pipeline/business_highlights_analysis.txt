ASML reports one segment, so the business lines below are its reported system technologies and its service and field option sales, with 2025 compared against 2024.

**Net system sales (total):** Net system sales rose 12.4% to €24,474.3 million from €21,768.7 million, while units fell to 535 from 583. The revenue gain came from a higher-value mix, since the units decline was concentrated in older DUV technologies and EUV and immersion volumes rose.

**KrF:** KrF sales fell 49.7% to €1,001.3 million from €1,991.2 million, and units dropped to 78 from 152. The annual report attributes the lower DUV system volumes to a decrease in sales volumes of other DUV lithography systems, without giving a separate cause for KrF.

**ArF dry:** ArF dry sales fell 44.9% to €427.0 million from €774.4 million, and units dropped to 16 from 28. The decline is part of the same lower-volume pattern in other DUV systems described in the annual report.

**I-line:** I-line sales fell 16.8% to €307.3 million from €369.2 million, and units fell to 54 from 65. The annual report does not give a specific cause for the I-line decline beyond the lower volumes of other DUV systems.

**ArF immersion:** ArF immersion sales rose 6.7% to €10,311.4 million from €9,667.0 million, on 131 units compared with 129. The annual report links the growth in system sales to greater NXE and NXT immersion adoption, which lifted revenue on a broadly flat unit count.

**EUV (NXE and EXE combined):** EUV sales rose 39.4% to €11,602.7 million from €8,321.4 million, and EUV systems recognized rose to 48 from 44. The growth reflects strong advanced Logic and DRAM demand for AI infrastructure, and EXE systems being delivered to and installed at more customers; the NXE line alone rose to €10,445.8 million from €7,856.4 million and the EXE line to €1,156.9 million from €465.0 million.

**Metrology & Inspection:** Metrology and inspection sales rose 27.8% to €824.6 million from €645.5 million, on 208 units compared with 165. The annual report does not state a specific cause for this increase.

**Net service and field option sales:** Service and field option sales rose 26.2% to €8,193.0 million from €6,494.2 million, and now make up 25.1% of total net sales compared with 23.0% in 2024. The increase came from the growing installed base of systems, higher levels of lithography tool use for certain customers, and more NXE field upgrades, and management expects another year of growth in 2026.

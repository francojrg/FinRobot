Operating cash flow reached €12,658.5m in 2025, up 13.4% from €11,166.2m in 2024 and €5,443.4m in 2023, but receivables factoring of €6,212.5m (2024: €2,042.7m, including €3,507.7m of down-payment receivables) supported part of that figure. Investing outflows of €3,777.8m included a €1,302.2m equity investment in Mistral AI. Financing outflows rose to €8,670.5m from €2,832.1m, driven by €5,950.0m of buybacks (2024: €500.0m), €2,550.3m of dividends and €1,066.7m of debt repayment. Cash and cash equivalents ended at €12,916.0m, up €180.1m. Shareholder distributions now depend on sustained cash generation, since €1,694.8m of debt principal falls due in 2026 and the new buyback of up to €12bn runs to 2028.

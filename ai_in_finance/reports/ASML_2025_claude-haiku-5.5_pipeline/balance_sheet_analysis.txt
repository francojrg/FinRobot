ASML's 2025 balance sheet is less liquid than in 2024 but carries less debt. Current assets were €30.6bn against current liabilities of €24.3bn, a current ratio of 1.26x, down from 1.53x in 2024 (calculated from the balance sheet table). Current liabilities rose 21%, mainly because current deferred revenue increased to €16.0bn from €12.6bn, and this line is now 66% of current liabilities. Total debt fell to €4.4bn from €4.7bn, and debt-to-equity eased to 0.22x from 0.25x. Equity rose 6.1% to €19.6bn as retained earnings grew €3.2bn, while €5.95bn of buybacks lifted treasury stock to €2.25bn. Cash and short-term investments of €13.3bn exceed total debt. Receivables fell to €3.0bn after €6.2bn was factored in 2025, so the liquidity ratio should be read with that factoring in mind.

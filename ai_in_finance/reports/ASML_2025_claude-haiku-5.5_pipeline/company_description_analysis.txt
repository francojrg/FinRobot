**Company overview and industry**

Founded in 1984, ASML Holding N.V. is a semiconductor equipment maker that provides holistic lithography solutions, software and services to microchip manufacturers. Its one reportable segment covers lithography, metrology and inspection systems. In 2025, net sales were €32.7bn, up 15.6% from €28.3bn in 2024. The company operates in the semiconductor equipment market, where demand depends on chipmakers' capacity spending.

**Core strengths and key products**

Products include EUV systems (NXE and EXE), ArF immersion, ArF dry, KrF and i-line lithography, and metrology and inspection systems. In 2025, net system sales were €24.5bn: NXE €10.4bn (44 units), ArF immersion €10.3bn (131 units) and metrology and inspection €0.8bn (208 units). Net service and field option sales were €8.2bn, or 25.1% of net sales, growing 26.2%. Gross margin was 52.8%, up from 51.3%. ASML relies on a single supplier, Carl Zeiss SMT, for optical columns; ASML holds a 24.9% equity interest in it.

**End markets, customers and market position**

In 2025, China (€9.5bn), Taiwan (€8.3bn) and South Korea (€8.2bn) were the largest sales regions, followed by the US (€4.1bn). Four customers each exceeded 10% of net sales, totaling €20.0bn, or 61.2%. The resources do not name these customers or state whether they are blue-chip companies. The resources also contain no market share data.

**Industry trends, opportunities and challenges**

AI demand drives advanced Logic and DRAM. Management expects the semiconductor market to grow at a 9% CAGR from 2025 to 2030, surpassing $1 trillion. Export controls, Chinese rare earth restrictions and supplier concentration are the main challenges. Both export-control measures are suspended until November 10, 2026.

**Strategic initiatives and response**

In September 2025, ASML invested €1.3bn in Mistral AI, gaining an 11.1% fully diluted stake. The company returned €8.5bn to shareholders in 2025, including €5.95bn of buybacks. In January 2026 it announced a buyback of up to €12bn through 2028. It proposed a 2025 dividend of €7.50 per share, up 17.2%.

**Summary**

ASML's 2025 sales rose 15.6% to €32.7bn, driven by services and EUV, with gross margin at 52.8%. Memory sales fell €0.2bn and system units dropped to 535 from 583. Four customers provided 61.2% of sales, and export controls and single-source suppliers remain key risks. Management guides 2026 revenue to €34–39bn, and the outlook depends on AI-driven capacity spending. The company has returned cash through dividends and buybacks.

**Total 2025 results:** Total net sales rose 15.6% to €32.7 billion (2024: €28.3 billion). Cost of sales rose 11.9% to €15.4 billion, R&D rose 9.2% to €4,698.8 million, and SG&A rose 7.9% to €1,257.8 million. Net income rose 26.9% to €9,609.4 million (2024: €7,571.6 million).

**Segments identified:** The filing reports two revenue lines, net system sales and net service and field option sales. It describes two market segments, Logic and Memory. It also reports two equity investments: Carl Zeiss SMT (equity method) and Mistral AI (equity investment).

*Note: Segment gross margins are derived as (net sales minus cost of sales) divided by net sales, from the Operating results table. They are illustrative calculations, not reported figures.*

**Net system sales**
Net system sales rose 12.4% to €24,474.3 million (2024: €21,768.7 million), although lithography units fell to 327 from 418. Cost of system sales rose 9.4% to €11,384.0 million, lifting the derived system gross margin to 53.5% from 52.2%. Growth came from NXE and NXT immersion adoption and EXE deliveries; lower other DUV volumes offset part of it.

**Net service and field option sales**
Net service and field option sales rose 26.2% to €8,193.0 million, lifting their share of net sales to 25.1% from 23.0%. Drivers were the installed base, higher tool use at certain customers and NXE field upgrades. Cost of these sales rose 19.7% to €4,025.3 million, a derived margin of 50.9% against 48.2%. Management expects growth again in 2026.

**Logic (market segment)**
Logic net sales rose €2.9 billion in 2025, primarily from leading-edge foundry growth for AI demand and customers building capacity for their next nodes. The filing gives no absolute Logic sales figure and no market share. Management expects Logic-related revenues to remain strong in 2026.

**Memory (market segment)**
Memory net sales decreased €0.2 billion. The filing links this to high Memory demand following strong 2024 growth, supported by high-bandwidth memory and DDR5 investment for AI applications. Management expects Memory revenues to be significantly higher in 2026. The filing gives no Memory market share.

**Equity partnerships (Carl Zeiss SMT and Mistral AI)**
Carl Zeiss SMT, a 24.9% equity-method investee and the single supplier of optical columns, contributed €216.7 million of profit in 2025 (2024: €209.8 million) and paid a €320.5 million dividend. ASML invested €1.3 billion in Mistral AI's Series C round, holding 11.1% fully diluted at year end. Trade restrictions are a stated risk, but the filing quantifies no policy effect on sales.

**Company Overview**

Founded in [year not disclosed in the provided resources], Rubrik, Inc. is a Palo Alto, California-based data security company that provides Zero Trust Data Security for cyber resilience and AI adoption. Its Rubrik Security Cloud (RSC) suite is a cloud-native SaaS platform that secures data across enterprise, cloud, SaaS, unstructured data, and identity providers. Its Rubrik Agent Cloud (RAC), commercially available since February 2026, is an AI operations platform for monitoring and controlling agentic actions. The company operates in the data security and cyber resilience market, where it sells subscriptions mainly through a sales team and partner network using a land-and-expand model.

**Core Strengths and Competitive Advantages**

Rubrik combines backup and recovery with cybersecurity on a single SaaS architecture. Its self-describing time-series data and metadata, proprietary data threat engine, and Preemptive Recovery Engine support cyber recovery. Native immutability and a logical air gap support its Zero Trust design. Key products include Data Protection, Data Threat Analytics, Identity Security, and Cyber Recovery. The company holds 425 issued U.S. patents and patents in various non-U.S. jurisdictions. Subscription ARR reached $1.46 billion (up 34%), Cloud ARR $1.29 billion (up 48%), and average net retention exceeded 120%. Customers with at least $100,000 in Subscription ARR rose 25% to 2,805.

**Industry Trends, Opportunities, and Challenges**

Opportunities include AI-driven threat escalation, enterprise AI adoption, cloud and SaaS migration, and data regulation such as GDPR. Challenges include customer cost-consciousness that has lengthened sales cycles, chip shortages and higher server prices, a net loss of $348.8 million, and an accumulated deficit of $3.19 billion.

**Recent Strategic Initiatives**

The company launched RAC in February 2026 and introduced the Ruby AI agent. It acquired Predibase in July 2025 for $109.1 million to accelerate agentic AI. It issued $1.15 billion of 0% convertible notes due 2030 and repaid its $327.9 million credit facility. It expanded its Microsoft partnership to co-engineer Azure products and uses a Direct-to-Distributor model with Supermicro. Free cash flow improved to $237.8 million from $21.6 million.

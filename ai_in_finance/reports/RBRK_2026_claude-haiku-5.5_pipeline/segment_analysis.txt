**Segment Identification and Scope**

The filing does not report separate reportable segments. The MD&A presents consolidated results and splits revenue into two categories, Subscription and Other, so this analysis uses those two categories. Segment-level net profit, named strategic partners, and market-share data are not disclosed in the provided material. Consolidated net loss narrowed to $348.8 million from $1,154.8 million.

**Subscription Revenue**

Subscription (RSC and term licenses): Revenue rose 53% to $1,263.9 million from $828.7 million, adding $435.2 million, driven by Subscription ARR growth of 34% to $1.46 billion and about $70.2 million of material-rights revenue. Gross profit rose 69% to $1,034.2 million; gross margin reached 82% versus 74%, though hosting costs increased $26.9 million with SaaS adoption.

**Other Revenue**

Other (professional services, perpetual licenses, appliances): Revenue fell 10% to $52.3 million from $57.8 million, now 4% of total revenue versus 7%. Cost fell 37% to $32.1 million, lifting gross profit 184% to $20.1 million and gross margin to 39% from 12%, driven by a $15.0 million stock-based compensation decrease and lower appliance costs tied to the contract-manufacturer transition.

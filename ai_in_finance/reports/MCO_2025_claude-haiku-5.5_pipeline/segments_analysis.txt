## Moody's Analytics (MA)

Restructuring charges rose to $77 million from $42 million, and banking transaction revenue fell 18% as MA continued its shift to cloud-hosted subscriptions. Still, external revenue rose 9% to $3,599 million (2024: $3,295 million), and organic constant currency growth was 7%. Adjusted operating income rose 18% to $1,196 million, with margin up 240 bps to 33.1%. Segment net profit is not disclosed.

## Moody's Investors Service (MIS)

FIG revenue grew only 4%, and U.S. FIG revenue fell $2 million on lower infrequent insurance-sector issuance. External revenue still rose 9% to $4,119 million, led by CFG up 9% to $2,132 million on higher investment-grade and high-yield issuance. Adjusted operating income rose 15% to $2,746 million; margin reached 63.6%, up 350 bps, as operating and SG&A expense fell 1%.

**Company Profile**

Founded year: not stated in the provided resources. MIS has operated for more than 115 years. Moody's Corporation is a global integrated risk assessment firm that provides credit ratings, research, proprietary data, analytical models and cloud-based decision tools to banks, insurers, investors, corporations and governments. It reports two segments. Moody's Analytics (MA) generated $3,599 million of external revenue in 2025, and Moody's Investors Service (MIS) generated $4,119 million. Total 2025 revenue was $7,718 million, up 9% from $7,088 million in 2024. The company employed about 16,000 people across more than 40 countries.

**Core strengths and products**

- Curated proprietary data and validated models, embedded in customer workflows. MA's total ARR was $3,498 million at December 31, 2025, up 8%.
- Decision Solutions (Banking, Insurance, KYC) is the fastest-growing MA line, with revenue up 12% to $1,692 million.
- MIS adjusted operating margin was 63.6% in 2025, compared with 51.1% for the group.

**Market position**

The resources do not disclose market share. MIS competes with other credit rating agencies and with investment banks and brokerages. MA competes with diversified financial information and enterprise risk software providers. MA sells significantly to banks, insurers and other financial services providers. The resources give no customer concentration figures.

**Geography:** U.S. revenue was $4,171 million (54% of total) and non-U.S. revenue was $3,547 million, of which EMEA was $2,376 million.

**Trends, opportunities and challenges**

Opportunities include Gen AI and agentic AI, private credit, and growth in debt issuance. Challenges include interest-rate and credit-spread sensitivity of ratings volumes, foreign exchange (about 39% of 2025 revenue was non-U.S. dollar), and new EU rules: the ESG Rating Regulation (applicable July 2026) and the AI Act.

**Recent initiatives**

Acquisitions include Praedicat (Q3 2024), Numerated (Q4 2024), CAPE Analytics (Q1 2025) and ICR Chile. The company divested MA Learning Solutions for a $23 million gain. Its Strategic and Operational Efficiency Restructuring Program cost $108 million in 2025. In October 2025 the board authorized $4.0 billion in share repurchases.

---

**Summary**

Moody's 2025 revenue rose 9% to $7,718 million, and adjusted operating margin expanded 300 basis points to 51.1%. Adjusted diluted EPS rose 20% to $14.94. MIS, which generates most of the revenue, depends on debt issuance volumes, although annual fee arrangements partly offset that dependence. MA's recurring revenue grew 11%, but KYC ARR (15%) trailed its organic constant currency recurring revenue growth (18%) because of customer attrition. Foreign exchange and new EU regulation remain the main risks to monitor.

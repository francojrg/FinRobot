**Company Overview**

Founded in 2001, Mastercard Incorporated is a technology company in the global payments industry. It was incorporated in Delaware in May 2001, and its principal operating subsidiary, Mastercard International, was formed in 1966. The company operates a four-party payments network that switches (authorizes, clears and settles) transactions in more than 150 currencies and more than 220 countries and territories. It does not issue cards, extend credit, or set the rates that issuers charge account holders or acquirers charge merchants.

**Core Strengths and Products**

Net revenue was $32.8 billion in 2025, up 16% from $28.2 billion in 2024. The payment network produced $19.5 billion (up 12%), and value-added services and solutions produced $13.3 billion (up 23%). The company cites its global network, its franchise model (including a settlement guarantee backed by its credit standing), multi-rail capabilities, brands, data and AI, and local presence as its competitive advantages.

**Market Position, Geography and Customers**

The filing does not quantify market share. It states that some competitors, including Visa, American Express, JCB, China UnionPay and Discover, have more share in certain jurisdictions. U.S. Mastercard-branded GDV grew 6% in 2025, compared with 10% for the rest of the world (U.S. dollar basis). No country other than the United States generated more than 10% of net revenue. The five largest customers generated about $6.9 billion, or 21%, of net revenue in 2025. The filing does not name them.

**Industry Trends, Opportunities and Challenges**

Opportunities include cash displacement, tokenization (about 40% of transactions in 2025), agentic commerce and stablecoin settlement. Challenges include government-backed real-time systems such as Brazil's PIX, FedNow and India's UPI, central bank digital currencies, interchange caps in the U.S. and New Zealand, and conflicting U.S. court rulings on the Regulation II cap.

**Strategic Initiatives**

In 2025 the company launched Mastercard Agent Pay, Threat Intelligence (with Recorded Future), Commerce Media and Merchant Cloud. It also became naming partner of the McLaren Formula 1 Team from the 2026 season. Acquisitions added 1 percentage point to 2025 net revenue growth. The company returned $17.6 billion to stockholders, including $11.7 billion of repurchases.

---

**Summary**

Mastercard grew 2025 net revenue 16% to $32.8 billion, with value-added services growing 23%. Its growth now depends on diversifying beyond the payment network, which grew 12%. Risks include government-backed payment systems, regulatory interchange caps, and litigation charges of $504 million. The filing gives no market share figure, and the five largest customers provide 21% of revenue. Adjusted operating expenses rose 14%. The company's ability to sustain value-added growth while managing regulation and cost is the key test to monitor.

Operating cash flow reached $17.6 billion in 2025, up $2.9 billion from $14.8 billion in 2024, mainly on higher net income, although the working-capital drag widened to $1.4 billion from $1.0 billion. Investing outflows fell to $1.4 billion from $3.4 billion because 2024 included $2.5 billion of acquisitions; capex was $1.2 billion. Financing outflows rose to $14.2 billion from $10.8 billion as debt proceeds fell to $1.2 billion from $4.0 billion, while buybacks took $11.7 billion and dividends $2.8 billion. Those distributions, $14.5 billion (illustrative: about 82% of operating cash flow), were funded internally. Cash, equivalents and investments rose to $10.9 billion, with $8.0 billion of undrawn credit against $19.0 billion of debt. Liquidity is adequate, but buybacks absorb most free cash, leaving little margin if litigation payments rise.

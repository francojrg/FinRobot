**Segment basis and scope.** The MD&A reports revenue, cost of revenue, and gross profit for four lines: Product, Installation, Service, and Electricity. Net profit is reported only at the company level, and the filing gives no segment market share figures. Company net loss was $(88.4) million in 2025 versus $(29.2) million in 2024, a $59.2 million widening. Gross profit rose $182.8 million, but operating expenses rose $132.9 million to $514.6 million, and other expense increased to $(157.2) million from $(49.3) million, including a $66.2 million debt conversion inducement expense and a $40.4 million equity loss from unconsolidated affiliates.

**Product**

Product revenue rose $446.1 million (41.1%) to $1,531.3 million, and cost of product revenue rose 44.8% to $992.8 million. Gross profit gained $139.1 million to $538.4 million, but margin fell to 35% from 37%. Charges included $21.9 million of Electrolyzer reserves and impairments, a $12.7 million construction-in-progress impairment, and a $15.9 million reduction in revenue from share-based consideration to a hyperscaler customer. Demand was largely attributable to a hyperscaler project facilitated through the Brookfield joint venture.

**Installation**

Installation revenue rose $81.8 million (66.8%) to $204.1 million, and cost rose $76.5 million (59.1%) to $205.9 million, both driven by the timing of project milestones for time-to-power sites. The gross loss narrowed $5.3 million to $(1.9) million, a margin of (1)% versus (6)% in 2024, so the segment remains loss-making.

**Service**

Service revenue rose $14.8 million (6.9%) to $228.3 million, driven by $18.6 million more maintenance-contract revenue. Cost fell $9.6 million (4.5%) to $205.4 million, as lower field replacement unit deployment saved $29.4 million. Gross profit swung $24.3 million to $22.9 million (10% margin) from $(1.4) million. Cost increases included $11.1 million of repair and overhaul costs from fleet aging, $6.4 million of maintenance materials, and $4.2 million of performance guarantee costs.

**Electricity**

Electricity revenue rose $7.5 million (14.2%) to $60.4 million, predominantly from a one-time settlement of a customer contract after redeploying assets for a partner. Cost fell $6.5 million (16.8%) to $32.4 million, mainly from fewer installed units. Gross profit rose $14.0 million to $27.9 million, a 46% margin versus 26%, and the one-time settlement accounts for most of the gain.

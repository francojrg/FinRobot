Bloom's 2025 cash generation was thin, and its liquidity came from financing. Operating cash flow was $113.9 million, up $22.0 million from $92.0 million in 2024, despite a $217.3 million working-capital outflow. Capital expenditures of $56.8 million left illustrative free cash flow (operating cash flow less capex) of about $57.2 million, versus $33.1 million in 2024. Financing added $1,508.4 million, mainly from $2,500.0 million of 0% convertible notes due 2030, while debt repayment totaled $987.2 million. Unrestricted cash rose to $2,454.1 million from $802.9 million, but recourse debt also rose to $2,613.7 million from $1,124.7 million. The balance therefore depends on refinancing rather than operations, and the Fremont expansion to 2 GW by end-2026 will draw on it.

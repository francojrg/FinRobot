# Segment Analysis: Streaming Revenue by Region, 2025 vs. 2024

The filing reports one streaming business and discloses revenue, but not operating profit, by region. The four regions below are treated as segments. Profit is shown at the company level only, so segment net profit cannot be stated.

## United States and Canada (UCAN)

UCAN revenue rose 15% to $19.96 billion from $17.36 billion, an increase of $2.60 billion, and its share of total streaming revenue edged down to 44.2% from 44.5%. Constant-currency growth was also 15%, so currency did not affect the result. UCAN operating profit is not disclosed.

## Europe, Middle East, and Africa (EMEA)

EMEA revenue rose 17% to $14.51 billion from $12.39 billion, an increase of $2.13 billion, and its share rose to 32.1% from 31.8%. Constant-currency growth was 16%, so currency added about one point. EMEA operating profit is not disclosed.

## Latin America (LATAM)

LATAM revenue rose 11% to $5.36 billion from $4.84 billion, an increase of $0.52 billion, and its share fell to 11.9% from 12.4%. Constant-currency growth was 23%, so currency reduced reported growth by about 12 points. Non-income tax assessments in Brazil raised other cost of revenues by $1,116 million at the company level. The filing does not allocate this charge by region, so its effect on LATAM profit cannot be measured.

## Asia-Pacific (APAC)

APAC revenue rose 21% to $5.35 billion from $4.41 billion, an increase of $0.94 billion, the fastest reported growth of the four regions. Its share rose to 11.8% from 11.3%. Constant-currency growth was 22%. APAC operating costs and margins are not disclosed.

## Company-Level Items Not Allocated to Regions

Total revenue rose 16% to $45.18 billion, and operating income rose 28% to $13.33 billion, lifting operating margin to 29.5% from 26.7%. Net income rose 26% to $10.98 billion. The pending WBD transaction, with a total equity value of about $72.0 billion and enterprise value of about $82.7 billion, is not assigned to any region.

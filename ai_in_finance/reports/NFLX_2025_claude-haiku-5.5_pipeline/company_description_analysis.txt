**Company overview and industry**

The founding year is not stated in the provided resources. Netflix, Inc. is one of the world's leading entertainment services, offering TV series, films, games and live programming across genres and languages on monthly membership plans. It reports as one operating segment. Revenue was $45.18 billion in 2025, up 16% from $39.00 billion in 2024 (17% in constant currency). It competes for consumers' leisure time with linear TV, pirated streaming, video games, user-generated content platforms and social media.

**Core strengths and products**

Operating income rose 28% to $13.33 billion, and operating margin reached 29.5%, against 26.7% in 2024 and 20.6% in 2023. Net income was $10.98 billion, up 26%. Paid plans range from $1 to $37 per month by country, and the company offers an ad-supported plan. It operates its own Open Connect content delivery network. Netflix stopped reporting membership counts in 2025, so this filing does not allow member growth or market share to be measured.

**Market position**

Streaming revenue by region in 2025 (share of total is an illustrative calculation from reported figures): UCAN $19.96 billion (about 44%, up 15%); EMEA $14.51 billion (about 32%, up 17%); LATAM $5.36 billion (about 12%, up 11%, or 23% in constant currency); APAC $5.35 billion (about 12%, up 21%). The filing names no major customers, because revenue comes from individual members. Market share is not disclosed.

**Trends, opportunities and challenges**

Regulation is expanding: countries are adding investment obligations, levies and content quotas, and some restrict ownership rights. Brazil's non-income tax assessments drove a $1,116 million increase in other cost of revenues. Content amortization rose $1,121 million, and content obligations total $24.04 billion. Foreign exchange losses were $123 million net of hedging. Growth in advertising revenue and in APAC and LATAM is the main offsetting opportunity.

**Strategic initiatives**

- **WBD acquisition:** Agreed December 4, 2025, and amended January 19, 2026. Holders receive $27.75 in cash per share, for an equity value of about $72.0 billion and an enterprise value of about $82.7 billion. Closing is expected 12 to 18 months after December 4, 2025. The bridge facility was raised to $42.2 billion.
- **Capital returns:** 86.5 million shares were repurchased for $9.1 billion in 2025, with $8.0 billion of authorization remaining.
- **Deleveraging:** Debt fell to $14.46 billion from $15.58 billion, after $1.83 billion of repayments.

---

**Summary**

Netflix grew 2025 revenue 16% to $45.18 billion and lifted operating margin to 29.5%, but cost pressures were real: content amortization and Brazilian tax assessments increased costs. The pending $82.7 billion-enterprise-value WBD acquisition is the dominant strategic issue. The thesis would be weakened if WBD financing terms worsen materially or if Netflix's margin fails to hold while integration costs rise.

Operating cash flow rose to $10.15 billion in 2025 from $7.36 billion in 2024, but financing outflows of $10.35 billion exceeded it. Share repurchases of $9.13 billion (2024: $6.26 billion) and debt repayments of $1.83 billion (2024: $0.40 billion, with no new issuance in 2025 versus $1.79 billion in 2024) drove the outflow. Free cash flow of $9.46 billion, after $688 million of capital expenditure (2024: $440 million), covered buybacks but not buybacks plus repayments. Net investment inflows of $1.75 billion closed the gap. Liquidity remains adequate, with $9.07 billion of cash, restricted cash and short-term investments and an undrawn $3 billion revolver. The $42.2 billion WBD bridge commitment is the main test: the buyback pace is unsustainable if free cash flow stops covering it.

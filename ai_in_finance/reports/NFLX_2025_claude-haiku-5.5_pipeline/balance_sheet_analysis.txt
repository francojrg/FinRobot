Netflix's 2025 balance sheet shows lower leverage and roughly stable liquidity, with buybacks absorbing much of the cash flow. Current assets of $13.0 billion covered current liabilities of $11.0 billion, a current ratio of 1.19x (illustrative), down slightly from 1.22x in 2024. Total debt fell to $14.5 billion from $15.6 billion, lowering debt-to-equity to 0.54x from 0.63x. Equity rose to $26.6 billion, but tangible book value remains negative at -$6.2 billion because goodwill and intangibles total $32.8 billion, about 59% of assets. Treasury stock grew to $22.4 billion from $13.2 billion. The main risk is the pending WBD transaction: $42.2 billion of bridge commitments were undrawn at year-end, and drawing them at scale would reverse the deleveraging.

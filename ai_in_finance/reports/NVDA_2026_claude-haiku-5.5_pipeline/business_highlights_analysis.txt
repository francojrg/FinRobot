## Performance Highlights by Business Line

The 10-K reports two segments, Compute & Networking and Graphics. It reports the four markets (Data Center, Gaming, Professional Visualization, Automotive) only as growth rates, not dollar amounts, so the market entries below give percentages without dollar values.

### Reportable Segments

**Compute & Networking:** Revenue rose 67% to $193,479 million from $116,193 million, and operating income rose 57% to $130,141 million from $82,875 million in fiscal 2026 versus fiscal 2025. Operating income grew more slowly than revenue because the segment absorbed a $4.5 billion charge for H20 excess inventory and purchase obligations in the first quarter of fiscal 2026, after the U.S. government required a license for H20 exports to China.

**Graphics:** Revenue rose 57% to $22,459 million from $14,304 million, and operating income rose 80% to $9,156 million from $5,085 million. The 10-K attributes the revenue increase to sales of the Blackwell architecture and attributes the operating income increase to revenue growth.

### Markets

**Data Center (within Compute & Networking):** The company states it was effectively foreclosed from competing in China's data center compute market as of the end of fiscal 2026, yet Data Center revenue still rose 68% year over year. Compute revenue grew 59% on demand for the Blackwell platform, and networking revenue grew 142% on the introduction and ramp of NVLink compute fabric for GB200 and GB300 systems and on growth in Ethernet and InfiniBand platforms.

**Gaming (within Graphics):** Revenue rose 41% year over year, driven by strong Blackwell demand. Management expects supply constraints to be a headwind to Gaming in the first quarter of fiscal 2027 and beyond, and the 10-K also flags that Chinese limits on gaming time and game access could adversely affect this revenue.

**Professional Visualization (within Graphics):** Revenue rose 70% year over year, driven by exceptional demand for Blackwell and the launch of the DGX Spark. The 10-K names these two drivers and does not quantify their separate contributions.

**Automotive (within Compute & Networking):** Revenue rose 39% year over year, driven by continued adoption of the company's self-driving platforms. The provided data does not include an Automotive dollar figure or segment-level detail for this market.

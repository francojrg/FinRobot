**1. Company Overview**

Founded in 1993, NVIDIA Corporation is a data center scale AI infrastructure company that provides GPUs, CPUs, networking, systems, and the CUDA software platform for AI, gaming, professional visualization, and automotive. Fiscal 2026 (ended January 25, 2026) revenue was $215.9 billion, up 65%, and net income was $120.1 billion, up 65%. Compute & Networking supplied $193.5 billion, about 90% of revenue (illustrative calculation).

**2. Core Strengths and Competitive Advantages**

The filing points to the CUDA platform and more than 7.5 million developers using it as the main advantages. GPUs and networking power over 78% of the TOP500 supercomputers. Data Center networking revenue grew 142% in fiscal 2026. The filing gives no overall market share figure, so market position rests on these indicators.

**3. End Markets, Geography and Customers**

Revenue from customers headquartered outside the U.S. fell to 31% from 41% of revenue. Two direct customers accounted for 22% and 14% of fiscal 2026 revenue; the filing does not name them. China is the largest constraint. The company states it was effectively foreclosed from China's data center compute market at fiscal year-end. It recorded a $4.5 billion H20 charge in Q1 fiscal 2026, earned about $60 million of H20 revenue under licenses, and has generated no H200 revenue to date.

**4. Industry Trends, Opportunities and Challenges**

AI buildout depends on customers' access to data centers, energy, and capital. Open-source models deployed on competitors' platforms could reduce demand for NVIDIA products. Named competitors include AMD, Intel, Broadcom, and cloud providers designing their own chips. Gross margin fell to 71.1% from 75.0%, mainly from the shift from Hopper HGX systems to Blackwell full-scale data center solutions and the H20 charge.

**5. Strategic Initiatives and Response to Market Conditions**

Blackwell Ultra shipped from Q2 fiscal 2026, and Rubin production shipments are expected in the second half of fiscal 2027. NVLink Fusion lets hyperscalers and custom ASIC designers integrate their own chips with the platform. The company invested $17.5 billion in private companies and infrastructure funds and provided $3.5 billion in land, power, and shell guarantees. Share repurchases totaled $40.4 billion. Capital expenditures rose to $6.1 billion from $3.4 billion and are expected to increase in fiscal 2027. A investment and partnership agreement with OpenAI is being finalized, with no assurance of completion.

---

**Summary**

NVIDIA's fiscal 2026 growth came with margin and geographic pressure. Revenue rose 65% to $215.9 billion, but gross margin fell 3.9 points to 71.1%, reflecting the Hopper-to-Blackwell transition and a $4.5 billion H20 charge. Non-U.S. customers fell to 31% of revenue from 41%, and the company is effectively excluded from China's data center compute market. Two direct customers provided 36% of revenue. Operating cash flow was $102.7 billion, and buybacks totaled $40.4 billion. Evidence to watch: Rubin shipments in the second half of fiscal 2027, any H200 revenue, and gross margin recovery.

**Business segments:** NVIDIA reports two segments, Compute & Networking and Graphics (MD&A, Note 16). The provided resources do not quantify market share, so this analysis does not state share figures.

### Compute & Networking

Compute & Networking revenue rose 67% to $193.5 billion from $116.2 billion, and segment operating income rose 57% to $130.1 billion from $82.9 billion. A $4.5 billion H20 charge in Q1 fiscal 2026 partly offset that income growth, and operating margin fell to 67.3% from 71.3% (calculated). Data Center networking revenue grew 142% on NVLink for GB200 and GB300.

### Graphics

Graphics revenue rose 57% to $22.5 billion from $14.3 billion, and segment operating income rose 80% to $9.2 billion from $5.1 billion. Growth came from Blackwell architecture sales, and operating margin rose to 40.8% from 35.6% (calculated). Gaming revenue grew 41% on strong Blackwell demand, but management expects supply constraints to weigh on Gaming from Q1 fiscal 2027.

*Source: MD&A segment tables and discussion; margins are calculated as segment operating income divided by segment revenue.*

Operating cash flow rose to $102.7 billion in fiscal 2026 from $64.1 billion, and free cash flow (operating cash flow less capital expenditure) reached $96.7 billion, against $60.9 billion. Working capital was the main drag: receivables absorbed $15.4 billion and inventory $11.3 billion, versus $13.1 billion and $4.8 billion a year earlier, leaving operating cash flow $17.4 billion below net income of $120.1 billion. Investing outflows rose to $52.2 billion from $20.4 billion, led by $31.7 billion of net investment purchases, a $14.5 billion business purchase, and capex of $6.0 billion (up from $3.2 billion). Financing outflows of $48.5 billion were mainly buybacks of $40.1 billion; dividends were $974 million, and no debt was repaid after $1.25 billion was repaid a year earlier. Cash and marketable securities reached $62.6 billion. Reinvestment and receivables growth now absorb a larger share of operating cash, so collections, inventory turns, and the planned fiscal 2027 capex increase are the items to watch.

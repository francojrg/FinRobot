**Productivity and Business Processes**

Revenue rose 16% to $140.0 billion (FY2025: $120.8 billion), and operating income rose 20% to $83.9 billion (FY2025: $69.8 billion). Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud revenue grew 17% on higher revenue per user from Copilot and E5. Operating expenses rose 9% to $31.1 billion on AI talent, compute and sales investment; cost of revenue rose 12%.

**Intelligent Cloud**

Revenue rose 30% to $137.8 billion (FY2025: $106.3 billion), and operating income rose 28% to $57.0 billion from $44.6 billion. Azure and other cloud services grew 41%; server products rose 1%. Cost of revenue rose 44% to $57.9 billion on AI infrastructure, lowering gross margin to 58.0% from 62.2% (derived). The filing does not quantify OpenAI's contribution to this segment.

**More Personal Computing**

Revenue fell 1% to $54.1 billion from $54.6 billion, while operating income rose 2% to $14.4 billion from $14.2 billion. XBOX content and services fell 5% and XBOX hardware fell 29%; Search advertising revenue excluding traffic acquisition costs grew 12%. Cost of revenue fell 7%, while operating expenses rose 6% on XBOX impairment charges.

Microsoft's June 30, 2026 balance sheet shows heavier investment in property and equipment, thinner liquidity, and a stronger equity base. Current assets of $207.7 billion covered current liabilities of $168.8 billion, a current ratio of 1.23, down from 1.35 in 2025 and 1.77 in 2023. Cash, equivalents and short-term investments fell to $76.7 billion from $94.6 billion, so net debt rose to $19.4 billion from $12.9 billion, even though total debt fell to $56.8 billion from $60.6 billion. Net property and equipment rose 47% to $337.3 billion, and long-term debt fell to $31.1 billion from $40.2 billion. Equity grew 29% to $442.4 billion, raising equity to total assets to 58% from 55% and lowering debt to equity to 0.13 from 0.18.

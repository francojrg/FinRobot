**Digital Media**

Digital Media revenue rose 11% to $17.65 billion in fiscal 2025 from $15.86 billion in fiscal 2024, and Digital Media ARR reached $19.20 billion, up 11.5% year over year. Subscription revenue growth was driven by Creative Cloud Pro and other flagship apps, and by Acrobat, across all routes to market and geographies.

**Digital Experience**

Digital Experience revenue rose 9% to $5.86 billion from $5.37 billion in fiscal 2024, with subscription revenue up 11% to $5.41 billion. The increase was driven by strength in GenStudio solutions and Adobe Experience Platform and related apps.

**Publishing and Advertising**

Revenue fell 7% to $256 million from $275 million, and subscription revenue declined 4% to $106 million, making it the only one of the three segments that shrank. The segment contains legacy solutions, and the filing does not attribute the decline to a specific cause.

**Customer groups (Digital Media and Digital Experience subscription revenue)**

Creative & Marketing Professionals subscription revenue grew 11% to $16.30 billion, and Business Professionals & Consumers subscription revenue grew 15% to $6.50 billion. The Creative & Marketing growth came from Creative Cloud Pro and flagship apps, GenStudio solutions, and Adobe Experience Platform and related apps, while the Business Professionals & Consumers growth came from Acrobat.

*Note: Adobe will combine its three segments into a single reportable segment beginning in the first quarter of fiscal 2026, so these segment results will not be reported separately after that point.*

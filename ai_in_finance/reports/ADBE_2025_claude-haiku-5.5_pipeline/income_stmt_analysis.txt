**Revenue:** Revenue grew 11% to $23.77 billion, and subscription made up 96% of it. Digital Media added $1.79 billion and Digital Experience added $498 million.

**Costs:** Cost of revenue rose 8%, but subscription costs rose 13% on hosting and AI inferencing. Operating expenses were flat only because FY2024 included a $1.00 billion Figma termination fee. Excluding that fee, they rose about 10%.

**Margins:** Gross margin was 89.3% (FY2024: 89.0%). Operating margin was 36.6% (FY2024: 36.0%, excluding the fee). Net margin was 30.0% (FY2024: 25.9%).

**Earnings per share:** Diluted EPS rose 35% to $16.70, helped by 5% fewer diluted shares and an 18% effective tax rate, down from 20%.

**Watch items:** Net income growth of 28% overstates the trend, because the prior-year fee was not tax-deductible. Interest expense rose to $263 million from $169 million. If cost growth keeps exceeding revenue growth, margins would compress.

Operating cash flow rose 25% to $10.03 billion in fiscal 2025, from $8.06 billion, though fiscal 2024 absorbed the $1 billion Figma termination fee. Free cash flow was $9.85 billion after $179 million of capital expenditure. The weak point is capital allocation: share repurchases of $11.28 billion exceeded free cash flow by about $1.4 billion (illustrative), and $1.5 billion of debt was repaid. Cash and equivalents fell $2.18 billion to $5.43 billion, and working capital turned negative at $(37) million from $711 million. Liquidity is still adequate, with a $1.5 billion undrawn revolver and no commercial paper outstanding. The $1.9 billion Semrush acquisition, to be funded from cash on hand, will reduce that buffer further. The view would be wrong if operating cash flow stopped keeping pace with buybacks while cash and short-term investments kept declining.

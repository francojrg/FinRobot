**Company overview**

The provided resources do not state Adobe's founding year; the 10-K says only that its innovations have shaped digital media "for over four decades." Adobe Inc. is a software company that provides creative, productivity, and customer-experience platforms, including Acrobat, Creative Cloud, Firefly, Adobe Experience Platform, and GenStudio. Fiscal 2025 revenue was $23.77 billion, up 11% from $21.51 billion, and subscription revenue was 96% of the total.

**Core strengths and key products**

Digital Media, at $17.65 billion (74% of revenue), includes Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, After Effects, Acrobat, Adobe Express, and Firefly. Digital Experience, at $5.86 billion (25%), includes Experience Platform, Real-Time Customer Data Platform, Journey Optimizer, Marketo Engage, and GenStudio. The filing's main differentiator is commercially safe Firefly models trained on licensed and public-domain content, with intellectual property indemnification available for eligible enterprise workflows.

**Market position**

The resources do not disclose market share. They also do not name major customers or state customer concentration. By region, the Americas generated 59% of fiscal 2025 revenue ($14.12 billion, up 10%), EMEA 27% ($6.29 billion, up 13%), and APAC 14% ($3.36 billion, up 10%). The filing describes competition from software, AI, hardware, operating system, and social media companies, and notes limited barriers to entry.

**Trends, opportunities, and challenges**

Generative and agentic AI is the central trend. Adobe offers its own Firefly models alongside partner models. Total Adobe ARR reached $25.20 billion, up 11.5%. The main cost challenge is AI inferencing and hosting: subscription cost of revenue rose 13% to $2.03 billion. Other challenges include AI-first competitors and macroeconomic and tax uncertainty, including the OECD 15% global minimum tax.

**Strategic initiatives and response**

Adobe released Acrobat Studio in August 2025. It agreed to acquire Semrush for about $1.9 billion in cash, expected to close in the first half of fiscal 2026. It issued $2.0 billion of senior notes in January 2025 and repurchased $11.28 billion of stock in fiscal 2025. Effective the first quarter of fiscal 2026, it will combine its three segments into one.

---

**Summary**

Adobe grew revenue 11% to $23.77 billion and net income 28% to $7.13 billion in fiscal 2025, on a subscription model. Its main constraints are rising AI and hosting costs, which grew subscription cost of revenue 13%, and competition from AI-first tools. The Semrush acquisition and the segment merger are the largest strategic changes. The thesis would be weakened if Total Adobe ARR growth fell below the 11.5% reported for fiscal 2025, or if cost of subscription revenue kept growing faster than revenue.

**North America:** Net sales rose 10% to $426.3 billion in 2025 from $387.5 billion in 2024, and operating income rose 19% to $29.6 billion from $25.0 billion, so operating margin was about 6.9% in 2025 versus about 6.4% in 2024 (illustrative, calculated from the reported figures). The sales growth came mainly from higher unit sales, including third-party seller sales, plus advertising and subscription services, while fulfillment, technology and infrastructure, shipping, and other operating costs grew and partly offset the gain in operating income; foreign exchange reduced segment net sales by $454 million and operating income by $204 million.

**International:** Net sales grew 13% to $161.9 billion in 2025 from $142.9 billion in 2024 (10% excluding foreign exchange), and operating income increased 25% to $4.8 billion from $3.8 billion. The increase was driven primarily by higher unit sales and advertising sales, partially offset by higher fulfillment and shipping costs, and foreign exchange added $4.9 billion to net sales and $903 million to operating income.

**Amazon Web Services (AWS):** Net sales rose 20% to $128.7 billion in 2025 from $107.6 billion in 2024, and operating income rose 14% to $45.6 billion from $39.8 billion, so operating margin fell to about 35.4% from about 37.0% (illustrative, calculated from the reported figures). Sales growth reflected increased customer usage, partly offset by pricing changes driven mainly by long-term customer contracts, while technology infrastructure spending, primarily added to support AWS business growth, absorbed much of the sales gain, and foreign exchange reduced operating income by $341 million.

**Scope note:** The filing does not disclose segment net income, so operating income is used as the segment profit measure. It also does not quantify market share, so market position is not addressed. Strategic partnership details (the Anthropic investment and related gains) are reported at the consolidated level, not by segment.

**North America**

North America net sales grew 10% to $426.3 billion in 2025 (2024: $387.5 billion), and operating income grew 19% to $29.6 billion (2024: $25.0 billion), lifting operating margin from 6.4% to 7.0% (calculated). Unit sales, advertising, and subscriptions drove growth, partly offset by higher fulfillment, technology and infrastructure, and shipping costs.

**International**

International net sales rose 13% to $161.9 billion (2024: $142.9 billion). Operating income rose 25% to $4.75 billion (2024: $3.79 billion), but foreign exchange contributed $903 million of that gain; excluding it, operating income rose about 1.5% (illustrative calculation). Unit sales and advertising drove growth, offset by fulfillment and shipping costs. Q1 2026 guidance adds investment in quick commerce and sharper prices.

**AWS**

AWS net sales grew 20% to $128.7 billion (2024: $107.6 billion), but operating income grew 14% to $45.6 billion (2024: $39.8 billion), cutting operating margin from 37.0% to 35.4% (calculated). Management cites customer usage growth, partly offset by pricing changes from long-term contracts, and technology infrastructure spending to support AWS growth, which weighed on operating income.

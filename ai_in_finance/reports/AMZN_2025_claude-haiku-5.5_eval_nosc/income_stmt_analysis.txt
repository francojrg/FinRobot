**Revenue:** Net sales rose 12% to $716.9 billion from $638.0 billion. AWS (+20%) and International (+13%) grew faster than North America (+10%).

**Costs:** Cost of sales grew 9%, slower than sales, and fell to 49.7% of sales from 51.1%. Technology and infrastructure spending rose 23% and is the main cost pressure.

**Margins:** Gross margin reached 50.3% (48.9% in 2024). Operating margin rose to 11.2% (10.8%) despite $5.2 billion of FTC settlement and severance charges. AWS operating margin slipped to 35.4% from 37.0%.

**Earnings:** Diluted EPS rose to $7.17 from $5.53. Net income includes a $15.3 billion net gain in other income, so normalized income, up 7% to $65.4 billion, is the better measure of operating earnings growth.

**Outlook:** First-quarter 2026 operating income guidance of $16.5–21.5 billion compares with $18.4 billion a year earlier. The materials provide no competitor data, so no industry comparison is possible.

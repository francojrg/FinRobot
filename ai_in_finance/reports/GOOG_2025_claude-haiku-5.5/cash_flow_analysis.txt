Operating cash flow rose 31% to $164.7 billion in 2025, from $125.3 billion in 2024, but capital expenditures rose 74% to $91.4 billion from $52.5 billion, so free cash flow was nearly flat at $73.3 billion versus $72.8 billion. Investing outflows reached $120.3 billion (2024: $45.5 billion). Financing outflows narrowed to $37.4 billion from $79.7 billion because debt issuance of $64.6 billion exceeded $32.4 billion of repayments, while buybacks fell to $45.4 billion from $62.2 billion. Dividends rose to $10.0 billion from $7.4 billion. Net borrowing of $32.1 billion means part of the investment was externally funded. Cash and short-term securities total $126.8 billion, but purchase commitments are $149.1 billion, and management expects significantly higher 2026 infrastructure spending, so sustaining free cash flow is the key test.

**Media and Entertainment (M&E):** Revenue was $332 million in fiscal 2026, up 5% from $315 million in fiscal 2025, the slowest growth of the four product families. The 10-K attributes the increase to lower contra revenue from the new transaction model and to EBA offerings, so part of the growth reflects a change in how sales incentives are recorded rather than higher customer demand.

**Other:** Revenue was $125 million in fiscal 2026, up 6% from $118 million, adding $7 million. Other revenue consists of consulting, training, and other products and services, which the 10-K recognizes as the products are delivered and services are performed.

**AutoCAD and AutoCAD LT:** Revenue was $1,787 million in fiscal 2026, up 14% from $1,572 million, an increase of $215 million. Management attributes the growth to both AutoCAD and AutoCAD LT.

**Manufacturing (MFG):** Revenue was $1,379 million in fiscal 2026, up 16% from $1,189 million, an increase of $190 million. Management attributes the growth to MFG Collections, Fusion, and Inventor.

**Architecture, Engineering, Construction and Operations (AECO):** Revenue was $3,583 million in fiscal 2026, up 22% from $2,937 million, the largest dollar gain of any product family at $646 million. Management attributes the growth to AEC Collections, EBA offerings, and Autodesk Construction Cloud (now known as Forma for Construction).

**1. Revenue and profit.** Revenue rose 18% to $7.21 billion (fiscal 2025: 12%), and subscription supplied $1.03 billion of the $1.08 billion gain. Profit lagged. Operating income rose 17% to $1,578 million, but marketing and sales rose 19% to $2.37 billion and restructuring charges reached $216 million (fiscal 2025: $15 million), so operating margin stayed at 22%. Net income rose only 1% to $1.12 billion because tax expense climbed to $479 million from $272 million. **2. Segments and channels.** AECO grew 22% to $3.58 billion, half of revenue, while M&E grew 5% to $332 million. Direct revenue rose 78% to 63% of sales, and indirect revenue fell 26%. **3. Presentation risk.** The new transaction model moves Solution Provider incentives from contra revenue into operating expense, so part of reported growth reflects presentation rather than volume. **4. Watch point.** Non-GAAP operating margin reached 38% (fiscal 2025: 36%). A GAAP margin gain in fiscal 2027, as restructuring declines, would confirm this trend.

**Basis of analysis:** The filing does not report operating profit by segment. It reports revenue by product family, geography, and sales channel, and it does not quantify market share. Each section below is drawn from those disclosures, with the consolidated profit and cost base covered separately. Percentages in parentheses marked "illustrative" are my calculations from the reported dollar figures.

### 1. AECO (Architecture, Engineering, Construction and Operations)

AECO revenue rose 22% to $3,583 million from $2,937 million, the fastest of the four families, lifting its share of net revenue from 48% to 50% (illustrative). Drivers were AEC Collections, EBA offerings, and Autodesk Construction Cloud (now Forma for Construction). The fiscal 2025 Payapps acquisition added construction payment management.

### 2. AutoCAD and AutoCAD LT

Revenue rose 14% to $1,787 million from $1,572 million, below the company's 18% growth, so its share fell from 26% to 25% (illustrative). The filing credits growth in both AutoCAD and AutoCAD LT but gives no product-level breakdown or market share figures.

### 3. MFG (Manufacturing)

Revenue rose 16% to $1,379 million from $1,189 million, attributed to MFG Collections, Fusion, and Inventor. Growth trailed the 18% company total, so the share slipped from 19.4% to 19.1% (illustrative).

### 4. M&E (Media and Entertainment)

Revenue rose 5% to $332 million from $315 million, the slowest of the four families. Management credits lower contra revenue under the new transaction model, an accounting change that the filing says lifts calculated revenue growth, along with EBA offerings. Share fell from 5.1% to 4.6% (illustrative).

### 5. Geography (Americas, EMEA, APAC)

EMEA grew fastest, up 21% to $2,794 million, ahead of Americas at 17% to $3,178 million and APAC at 11% to $1,234 million (14% in constant currency). Foreign exchange was neutral to the 18% total growth. The filing says geopolitical conflict and trade wars have hurt, and may continue to hurt, results in Brazil, India, and China.

### 6. Sales Channel (Direct and Indirect)

Direct revenue rose 78% to $4,560 million, while indirect fell 26% to $2,646 million, lifting direct to 63% of revenue from 42%. The filing ties the shift to the new transaction model and the online store. TD Synnex fell to 14% of revenue from 33%.

### 7. Net Profit (Consolidated)

Net income rose 1% to $1,124 million from $1,112 million, far slower than GAAP operating income (up 17% to $1,578 million). Tax expense rose 76% to $479 million, so the effective rate was about 30% versus 20% (illustrative). Restructuring charges rose to $216 million from $15 million.

### 8. Cost Base (Consolidated)

Marketing and sales rose 19% to $2,373 million, mainly from Solution Provider commissions now recorded as operating expense under the new transaction model. R&D rose 11% to $1,643 million on headcount, merit increases, and cloud hosting. G&A rose 7% to $693 million, including higher charitable contributions to the Autodesk Foundation.

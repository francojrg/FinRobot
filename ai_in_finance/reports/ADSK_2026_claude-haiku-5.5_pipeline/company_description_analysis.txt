**Company profile**

**Overview.** The founding year is not stated in the provided resources. Autodesk, Inc. is a global 3D design, engineering, and entertainment technology company that provides software for architecture, engineering, and construction (AECO), manufacturing, and media and entertainment. Fiscal 2026 net revenue (year ended January 31, 2026) was $7.21 billion, up 18% from $6.13 billion, and 97% of it was recurring.

**Core strengths and products.** Subscription revenue was $6.74 billion in fiscal 2026. Key products include Revit, AutoCAD and AutoCAD LT, AutoCAD Civil 3D, Autodesk Build, Fusion, Inventor, Maya, and 3ds Max. Net revenue retention was above the 100%–110% range on a constant currency basis at both January 31, 2026 and 2025. Current remaining performance obligations (RPO) reached $5.48 billion, up 23%.

**Market position.** Fiscal 2026 revenue by region was Americas 44% ($3.18 billion), EMEA 39% ($2.79 billion), and APAC 17% ($1.23 billion); international sales are expected to remain the majority. TD Synnex accounted for 14% of revenue, down from 33% in fiscal 2025. No other distributor, reseller, or direct customer reached 10%. The resources do not contain market share data. Named competitors include Bentley Systems, Dassault Systèmes, Nemetschek, PTC, Procore, and Trimble, among others.

**Industry trends, opportunities, and challenges.** The industry is moving from desktop licenses to cloud and AI-enabled workflows. Autodesk's direct channel rose to 63% of revenue in fiscal 2026 from 42% in fiscal 2025. Challenges include low barriers to entry, AI that may "reduce customer demand" in unpredictable ways, geopolitical conflict and trade wars, the move to annual billing on multi-year contracts, and persistent unauthorized use.

**Strategic initiatives and response.** Under a new transaction model, Solution Providers quote prices while Autodesk contracts directly with the customer. Autodesk acquired Payapps in fiscal 2025 and completed no business combinations in fiscal 2026. Restructuring, other exit costs, and facility reductions were $216 million in fiscal 2026, versus $15 million in fiscal 2025, to reallocate resources to cloud, platform, and AI. The company repurchased 5 million shares for $1.40 billion.

**Summary**

Fiscal 2026 revenue grew 18% to $7.21 billion, recurring revenue reached 97% of the total, and operating cash flow rose to $2.45 billion from $1.61 billion. GAAP operating margin was flat at 22%, while non-GAAP margin rose to 38% from 36%. AECO grew fastest (up 22%), and direct revenue rose 78%, reducing TD Synnex's share to 14%. The main risks are higher marketing and sales expense from the new transaction model, billing and cash timing from the annual-billing transition, and AI-driven competition. The thesis would be wrong if billings and current RPO growth fall below revenue growth, or if GAAP margin does not improve as restructuring charges decline.

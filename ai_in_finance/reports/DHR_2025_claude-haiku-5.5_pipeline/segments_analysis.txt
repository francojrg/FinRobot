**Business segments:** Biotechnology, Life Sciences, and Diagnostics.

**Biotechnology**

Sales rose 8.0% to $7,293 million (core +6.5%), led by bioprocessing consumables, while equipment declined. Operating profit rose 10.6% to $1,864 million, and margin gained 70 basis points to 25.6% despite 130 basis points of 2025 impairments on technology, intangibles and a facility.

**Life Sciences**

Sales were flat at $7,334 million as core sales fell 1.5%, with lower funding at emerging biotech and academic customers. Operating profit fell 41% to $520 million from $879 million, and margin dropped 490 basis points to 7.1%. Impairments of a trade name and a facility cost 305 basis points, and cost deleverage, mix and productivity spending cost 190 basis points.

**Diagnostics**

Sales rose 1.5% to $9,941 million (core +1.5%), led by consumables. Price decreases of 1.0%, mainly from China volume-based procurement, and lower China core sales offset part of the growth. Operating profit rose 1.0% to $2,650 million, but margin slipped 10 basis points to 26.7%: cost deleverage, currency, mix and productivity spending cost 95 basis points, partly offset by lapping 2024 charges.

**Gaps in the resources**

- Net profit is not reported by segment; operating profit is the only segment profit measure provided.
- No market share data is provided, and the filing does not describe market position changes.
- No strategic partnerships are named. The filing describes acquisitions (including Abcam, which produced a 2024 inventory fair-value charge), the Veralto Separation, and the Swiss franc bond issuance, but none of these are partnerships.
- Product innovation is not quantified; the segment discussion cites only consumables and equipment sales trends.
- The MD&A gives diluted EPS from continuing operations of $5.03 for 2025, while the income statement table shows $5.05.

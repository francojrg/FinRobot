**Life Sciences:** Sales were flat at $7,334 million in 2025 compared with $7,329 million in 2024, as a 1.5% core sales decline offset currency and acquisition effects. Operating profit fell to $520 million from $879 million, and the operating margin dropped 490 basis points to 7.1% from 12.0%.

Of the margin decline, 305 basis points came from 2025 impairment charges on a trade name and a facility, net of a 2024 trade name impairment, and 190 basis points came from cost-structure deleverage, product mix, lower core sales and productivity-program costs. The core sales decline reflected lower funding at emerging biotechnology and academic and government customers and lower demand for plasmid and mRNA products at two large customers.

**Diagnostics:** Sales rose 1.5% to $9,941 million from $9,787 million in 2024, with core sales also up 1.5%, and operating profit increased to $2,650 million from $2,625 million, while the operating margin eased 10 basis points to 26.7%. Price decreases of 1.0% from China's volume-based procurement program and healthcare reimbursement changes, along with lower respiratory test demand in molecular diagnostics, held back growth, while higher non-respiratory molecular testing and clinical diagnostics sales, led by pathology, partly offset these declines.

**Biotechnology:** Sales increased 8.0% to $7,293 million from $6,759 million in 2024 (core sales up 6.5%), and operating profit rose to $1,864 million from $1,685 million, lifting the operating margin 70 basis points to 25.6%. Growth in the bioprocessing business was led by consumables demand from large pharmaceutical and CDMO customers, partly offset by lower equipment demand, while a 130-basis-point impairment charge on technology, other intangible assets and a facility limited the margin gain.

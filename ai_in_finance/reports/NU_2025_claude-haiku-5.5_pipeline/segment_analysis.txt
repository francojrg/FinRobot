**Basis note:** The 20-F does not report profit by business segment. The segments below are the revenue, funding, cost and geographic lines that the MD&A discusses. Consolidated net income was US$2,871.7 million in 2025, up 45.6% from US$1,972.1 million in 2024.

**1. Consumer Credit (Credit Cards and Loans)**
Interest income on loans rose 57.4% to US$4,784.3 million in 2025 from US$3,038.7 million in 2024, and credit card interest rose 20.9% to US$4,597.8 million. The cost is provisioning: expected credit loss grew 32.7% to US$4,204.9 million, while the ECL ratio on cards and loans held at 15.4% in both years. The view weakens if that ratio rises above 15.4%.

**2. Deposits and Funding**
Interest expense on deposits rose 65.5% to US$3,876.5 million, mainly because deposits grew 45.3% to US$41.9 billion (29.0% on an FX-neutral basis). In Brazil, the cost of funding was 81% of the interbank rate in December 2025, above the all-time low of 78% in December 2022.

**3. Card and Fee Income**
Credit and prepaid card income rose 24.6% to US$1,720.3 million on purchase volume of US$141.7 billion, up 13% reported (17% FX-neutral). Nu held 14.7% of Brazil's total credit card PV in 2025, against 14.2% in 2024 and 8.7% in 2021 (ABECS). Late fees rose 33.9% to US$385.0 million.

**4. Operating Cost Base (Shared Platform)**
Operating expenses rose 12.0% to US$2,752.9 million, but fell to 17.5% of revenue from 21.3%. General and administrative expenses rose 13.1%, marketing rose 22.9% on US$54.4 million more digital and traditional advertising, and customer support and operations rose 7.8%. Gross margin fell to 42.0% from 45.6%, reflecting the expected credit loss growth, and the effective tax rate fell to 25.8% from 29.4%, which management attributed to recognized deferred tax assets.

**5. International Expansion (Mexico, Colombia, United States)**
Colombian deposits reached US$2.5 billion at December 31, 2025, after the Cuenta Nu launch in Q2 2024. Mexico's GDP growth slowed to 0.6% in 2025 from 1.5%. Management is advancing the Mexico banking license and preparing U.S. operations after conditional bank charter approval, and it expects these moves to raise near-term operating costs.

**Strategic Partnerships and Acquisitions**
Nu acquired Nu Investimentos and Hyperplane (July 31, 2024), made a minority investment in Tyme Group (December 12, 2024), and runs NuCel as an MVNO partnership with Claro. Card fees are set by Mastercard. The 20-F does not quantify the revenue impact of any of these.

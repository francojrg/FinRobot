Total assets rose 50% to US$74.9 billion at December 31, 2025, from US$49.9 billion a year earlier, and equity rose 48% to US$11.3 billion, so equity stayed near 15% of assets (15.3% in 2024). The statement of financial position has no current/non-current split, so liquidity is judged against deposits. Cash and cash equivalents of US$15.0 billion covered about 36% of US$41.9 billion in deposits, up from about 32% (illustrative ratios). Deposits are 56% of assets, and borrowings and financing rose to US$4.4 billion from US$1.7 billion, about 0.39 times equity. Leverage is rising mainly through deposits and market borrowings, not equity. The view weakens if equity falls below 15% of assets, cash coverage of deposits drops, or the loss charge grows faster than revenue.

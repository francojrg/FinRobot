**Revenue:** Total revenue grew 37.0% to US$15,774.8 million. Interest income supplied 85.2% of it, and credit and prepaid card income rose 24.6% to US$1,720.3 million.

**Costs:** Costs of financial and transactional services rose 46.1%, faster than revenue, led by deposit interest (+65.5%) and expected credit loss (+32.7%).

**Margins:** Gross margin fell to 42.0% from 45.6%. Operating expenses eased to 17.5% of revenue from 21.3%. Net margin rose to 18.2% from 17.1% (derived), helped by a lower tax rate of 25.8% versus 29.4%.

**EPS:** Diluted EPS rose 44.9% to US$0.5846, while diluted shares rose only 0.4%.

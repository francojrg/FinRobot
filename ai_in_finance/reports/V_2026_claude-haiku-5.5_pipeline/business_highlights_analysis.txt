**Visa: performance by business line (fiscal 2026 vs. fiscal 2025)**

The filing does not report revenue or operating profit by business line, so the figures below come from the volume and revenue-category disclosures that the filing does provide.

**Consumer payments:** Total nominal payments volume grew 10% to $15.3 trillion for the twelve months ended June 30, 2026, and processed transactions rose 9% to 281.3 billion, which drove service and data processing revenue. Service revenue increased 14% and data processing revenue increased 17%, but international transaction revenue grew only 8% because of lower currency volatility and business mix, even though nominal cross-border volume (excluding intra-Europe) rose 15%.

**Commercial and money movement solutions (CMS):** Visa's nominal commercial payments volume rose 12% to $1.9 trillion for the twelve months ended June 30, 2026, and Visa Direct processed more than 15 billion transactions for more than 800 clients in fiscal 2026. The filing gives no prior-year transaction count for Visa Direct and no CMS revenue figure, so the contribution of this line to revenue and earnings cannot be isolated from the disclosures provided.

**Value-added services (VAS):** VAS revenue increased 31% to $14.2 billion from $10.9 billion, and it represented about 31% of fiscal 2026 net revenue of $45.9 billion (illustrative calculation: $14.2 billion ÷ $45.9 billion). Growth was across all portfolios, with the largest contributions from Issuing Solutions and Advisory and Other Services, supported by client consulting engagements up about 30%, higher marketing demand tied to the FIFA World Cup 2026 and the Winter Olympics, processed transactions up 9%, and payment credentials up 6%.

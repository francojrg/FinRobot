# Visa Inc. Segment Analysis, Fiscal 2026

**Basis of segmentation.** The resources do not present reportable segments with their own profit, cost, or market share data. The MD&A splits net revenue into U.S. and International, so this analysis uses those two groups. Share-of-revenue figures are my arithmetic from the reported totals (illustrative).

## U.S.

U.S. net revenue rose 11% to $17,423 million in fiscal 2026, up $1,790 million from $15,633 million, after 6% growth in fiscal 2025. U.S. nominal payments volume grew 8% to $7,338 billion (twelve months to June 2026), against 6% a year earlier. The U.S. share of net revenue fell to 38% from 39%. Segment profit is not disclosed.

## International

International net revenue rose 17% to $28,480 million, up $4,113 million from $24,367 million, after 15% growth in fiscal 2025. International nominal payments volume grew 12% to $8,000 billion (twelve months to June 2026). Its share of net revenue rose to 62% from 61%. Brazil's central bank rules, once approved, are expected to raise settlement exposure and collateral requirements.

## Items not allocated to segments

- **Costs:** Operating expenses were $17,793 million, up 11%. Personnel rose 14% to $7,934 million, and marketing rose 37% to $2,315 million, driven partly by the FIFA World Cup 2026 and the Milano Cortina 2026 Winter Olympics sponsorships. The litigation provision fell to $1,764 million from $2,562 million.
- **Profit:** Operating income was $29,874 million, up about 12% from $26,556 million. Net income was $23,504 million, up 17% from $20,058 million. Non-GAAP net income was $25,314 million, up 12%.
- **Tax:** A $314 million deferred tax benefit followed a change in U.S. taxation of certain foreign earnings. The OECD 15% global minimum tax produced a provision, primarily related to Singapore.
- **Acquisitions:** Prisma and Newpay in Argentina were acquired in February 2026 for $1.5 billion in cash. BioCatch was agreed in August 2026 for $2.4 billion in cash and remains subject to closing conditions.
- **Market share:** The resources do not quantify Visa's market share or its change.

**Test of the segment view:** The International growth lead is a reported fact for fiscal 2026. A reversal would appear if International net revenue growth fell below U.S. growth in fiscal 2027.

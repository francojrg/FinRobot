**Market Intelligence:** Revenue rose 6% to $4,916 million in 2025 from $4,645 million in 2024, and segment operating profit rose 13% to $991 million, lifting the operating margin to 20% from 19%. Subscription revenue grew 6% to $4,107 million, driven by Data, Analytics & Insights (helped by the Visible Alpha and With Intelligence acquisitions), workflow solutions in Enterprise Solutions, and RatingsXpress and RatingsDirect, partly offset by the August 2024 sale of Fincentric; operating profit absorbed $56 million of employee severance and $21 million of acquisition-related costs.

**Ratings:** Revenue increased 8% to $4,724 million, and operating profit increased 11% to $3,013 million, with the operating margin rising to 64% from 62%. Transaction revenue grew 6% to $2,470 million on higher corporate bond ratings and CLO-driven structured finance revenue, partly offset by lower bank loan ratings, while billed issuance rose 11% to $4,327 billion, with high-yield issuance up 17%.

**Energy:** Revenue increased 7% to $2,299 million, and operating profit increased 12% to $943 million, with the operating margin rising to 41% from 39%. Subscription revenue grew 8% to $2,016 million on continued demand for market data and insights under enterprise contracts, sales usage-based royalties rose 17% to $120 million on higher trading volumes for Platts-based contracts, and conference revenue increased with attendance at CERAWeek in 2025, while operating profit would have risen 9% excluding the $14 million of IHS Markit merger costs recorded in 2024 and by about 2 percentage points less for severance charges of $19 million in 2025.

**Mobility:** Revenue increased 9% to $1,747 million, and operating profit increased 21% to $378 million, with the operating margin rising to 22% from 19%. Growth came from the Dealer and Financial businesses, driven by new Dealer business, strong underwriting volumes, market share gains and improved contract terms, while non-subscription revenue grew only 5% because Manufacturing discretionary budgets tightened amid tariff and EV-adoption uncertainty and recall activity was lower. The operating profit gain was flattered by a 12-percentage-point Executive Leadership Team transition benefit and an 8-point legal settlement recovery; excluding these and other one-time items, operating profit rose 11%.

**Indices:** Revenue increased 14% to $1,850 million, and operating profit increased 15% to $1,271 million, with the operating margin rising to 69% from 68%. Asset-linked fees grew 15% to $1,206 million because ending ETF assets under management rose 25% to $5.480 trillion, while exchange-traded derivative royalties rose 12% to $324 million on higher trading volumes, so the largest contributor was the growth in asset levels rather than new index launches. Net operating profit, after $322 million attributable to noncontrolling interests, rose 16% to $949 million.

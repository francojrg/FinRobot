**Consolidated context.** Revenue rose 8% to $15,336 million in 2025 (2024: $14,208 million). Net income attributable to S&P Global rose 16% to $4,471 million, and diluted EPS rose 19% to $14.66 (2024: $12.35). Operating profit included a $270 million pre-tax gain on the October 2025 sale of OSTTRA, a 50/50 joint venture with CME Group, to KKR. The MD&A and tables provided do not quantify market share. The closest measures are Indices' ETF assets under management and Ratings' billed issuance.

**Market Intelligence**
Market Intelligence revenue rose 6% to $4,916 million (2024: $4,645 million), led by Data, Analytics & Insights subscriptions and the With Intelligence and Visible Alpha acquisitions, partly offset by the Fincentric sale. Operating profit rose 13% to $991 million, and margin widened to 20% from 19%. Operating-related expenses rose 3% to $2,082 million, mainly on compensation for merit increases and added headcount.

**Ratings**
Ratings revenue rose 8% to $4,724 million (2024: $4,370 million). Transaction revenue grew 6% to $2,470 million and non-transaction revenue grew 10% to $2,254 million. Operating profit rose 11% to $3,013 million, with margin at 64% versus 62%. Legal costs of $42 million reduced growth by 1 percentage point. Billed issuance rose 11% to $4,327 billion.

**Energy**
Energy revenue rose 7% to $2,299 million (2024: $2,142 million), with subscription revenue up 8% to $2,016 million and sales usage-based royalties up 17% to $120 million. Operating profit rose 12% to $943 million, with margin at 41% versus 39%. Excluding 2024 IHS Markit merger costs and 2025 severance of $19 million, growth was 9%. Operating-related expenses rose 5% to $735 million.

**Mobility**
Mobility revenue rose 9% to $1,747 million (2024: $1,609 million), led by the Dealer and Financial businesses. Non-subscription revenue rose only 5% to $325 million, held back by tighter Manufacturing discretionary budgets amid tariff and EV uncertainty and lower recall activity. Operating profit rose 21% to $378 million, helped by a $4 million Executive Leadership Team benefit and a $3 million legal recovery. Separation into a new public company is expected by mid-2026.

**Indices**
Indices revenue rose 14% to $1,850 million (2024: $1,628 million), driven by asset-linked fees up 15% to $1,206 million as ending ETF AUM rose 25% to $5.480 trillion. Sales usage-based royalties rose 12% to $324 million. Operating profit rose 15% to $1,271 million. After $322 million attributable to noncontrolling interests, net operating profit rose 16% to $949 million.

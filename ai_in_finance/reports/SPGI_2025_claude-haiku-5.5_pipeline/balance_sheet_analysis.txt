Total debt rose to $13.6 billion at December 31, 2025, from $11.9 billion a year earlier, while common equity fell to $31.1 billion from $33.2 billion. Total debt to common equity therefore moved from 0.36 to 0.44 (illustrative ratio). The equity decline traces mainly to treasury stock, which grew $4.7 billion to $36.4 billion, alongside $5.0 billion of 2025 share repurchases, funded in part by $1.0 billion of new senior notes and $715 million of commercial paper. Liquidity is tighter: current assets of $6.3 billion cover current liabilities of $7.6 billion, a current ratio of 0.82 against 0.85 in 2024, and working capital is negative $1.3 billion. Goodwill and intangibles total $52.7 billion, 86% of assets, leaving tangible book value at negative $21.6 billion. Free cash flow of $5.1 billion and a single 4-to-1 leverage covenant that has never been exceeded support solvency, but further debt-funded buybacks would weaken the equity base.

Operating cash flow was $5,651 million in 2025, down 1% from $5,689 million in 2024, on lower collections and higher compensation payments. Free cash flow fell 3% to $5,135 million as capital expenditures rose to $195 million from $124 million. Investing outflows of $704 million reflected the $1.8 billion With Intelligence acquisition, partly offset by $1.5 billion of OSTTRA proceeds. Share repurchases of $5,001 million and $1,170 million of dividends totaled about $6.2 billion (illustrative), exceeding free cash flow. The gap was covered by $993 million of new notes, $715 million of commercial paper outstanding, and disposal proceeds. The $2.0 billion credit facility and a 4-to-1 leverage covenant, never exceeded, leave room. Sustaining this payout beyond free cash flow, however, depends on disposals and borrowing.

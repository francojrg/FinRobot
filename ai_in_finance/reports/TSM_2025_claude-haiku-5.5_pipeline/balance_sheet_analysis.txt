At December 31, 2025, current assets of NT$3.82 trillion covered current liabilities of NT$1.52 trillion 2.51 times, up from 2.36 times in 2024 and 2.08 times in 2022. Total debt rose slightly to NT$1.06 trillion from NT$1.05 trillion, but debt-to-equity fell to 0.20 from 0.25 as stockholders' equity grew to NT$5.36 trillion. Total liabilities fell to 32% of total assets from 36%. Cash and short-term investments of NT$3.13 trillion exceed total debt by about NT$2.06 trillion. The pressure point is asset intensity: construction in progress rose to NT$1.52 trillion from NT$1.08 trillion, and 2026 capital expenditure guidance of US$52 to 56 billion could reverse the deleveraging if operating cash flow, NT$2.27 trillion in 2025, does not keep pace. Leverage is low. Debt growth outpacing equity growth would prove this view wrong.

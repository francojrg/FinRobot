(1) Net revenue rose 31.6% to NT$3,809,054 million, and net margin reached 44.6% (40.0%), with diluted EPS of NT$327.35 (NT$226.25). The main drag was currency: the NT dollar appreciated from NT$32.13 to NT$31.11 per US dollar, reducing revenue. Income tax expense rose 39.6% to NT$346,530 million, about 17% of pre-tax income (illustrative: NT$346,530 million ÷ NT$2,041,655 million). (2) Wafer fabrication, about 86% of revenue, drove results: HPC reached 58% of net revenue, up 48% to NT$2,192,931 million, while smartphone fell to 29% from 35%. (3) Advanced nodes at 7-nanometer and below rose to 74% of wafer revenue from 69%, with 3-nanometer at 24% from 18%, supporting gross margin of 59.9% (56.1%). R&D grew 20.7% to NT$246,427 million. (4) The risks are 2026 capex guidance of US$52–56 billion, long-term debt of NT$1,032,988 million, and carbon fees starting in 2026. The view would be wrong if advanced-node share stopped rising while smartphone share kept falling.

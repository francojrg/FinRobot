Leverage rose in 2025 while liquidity improved. Total debt climbed to $39.4 billion from $31.3 billion, and total debt to equity was 0.74, up from 0.63 in 2024 but below 0.75 in 2023. Goodwill and intangibles of $65.2 billion make up 59% of $110.3 billion in total assets (63% in 2024), and tangible book value remains negative at -$11.8 billion. Liquidity strengthened: the current ratio rose to 1.89 from 1.66 as current assets reached $28.7 billion against $15.2 billion in current liabilities, and cash nearly tripled to $9.9 billion. Equity grew to $53.4 billion from $49.6 billion. Net debt of $29.5 billion is the figure to watch for whether deleveraging follows.

**Life Sciences Solutions:** Revenue rose 8% to $10,374 million in 2025 from $9,631 million in 2024, with organic growth of 3%, and segment income rose 8% to $3,768 million, while segment margin slipped 0.1 point to 36.3%. Bioproduction drove the increase, growing $548 million on a reported basis on higher pharma and biotech demand and the September 2025 filtration and separation acquisition, while the margin decline reflected acquisition effects, unfavorable business mix and strategic investments, partly offset by very strong productivity improvements.

**Analytical Instruments:** Revenue was nearly flat, rising 1% to $7,554 million with no organic growth, while segment income fell 11% to $1,736 million and segment margin dropped 3.2 points to 23.0%, the weakest result of the four segments. Electron microscopy (up $87 million) and chromatography and mass spectrometry (up $83 million) grew, but chemical analysis declined $78 million, and the margin compression came from tariffs and related foreign exchange effects, strategic investments and unfavorable business mix, partly offset by strong productivity improvements.

**Specialty Diagnostics:** Revenue rose 4% to $4,676 million, with 2% organic growth, and segment income rose 8% to $1,256 million, lifting segment margin 1.2 points to 26.9%. Organic growth was led by the healthcare market channel and transplant diagnostics, and the margin gain was attributed to strong productivity improvements, while clinical diagnostics (up $52 million) and immunodiagnostics (up $48 million) also contributed to reported revenue growth.

**Laboratory Products and Biopharma Services:** Revenue grew 4% to $23,984 million, with 3% organic growth, and segment income rose 8% to $3,350 million, lifting segment margin 0.7 point to 14.0%; this segment was the largest contributor to organic revenue growth. Pharma services (up $457 million) and the research and safety market channel (up $422 million) drove growth, partly offset by moderation in COVID-19 vaccine and therapy activity, and the margin gain reflected exceptionally strong productivity improvements, partly offset by unfavorable business mix and strategic investments.

**Segments:** Life Sciences Solutions, Analytical Instruments, Specialty Diagnostics, and Laboratory Products and Biopharma Services. Figures are for 2025 versus 2024 and use the company's segment income measure, which excludes certain charges and credits.

**Analytical Instruments**

Segment income fell 11% to $1,736 million from $1,955 million, and margin dropped 3.2 points to 23.0% from 26.2%, on revenue up 1% to $7,554 million from $7,463 million with organic revenue flat. Tariffs and related foreign exchange drove the decline, with strategic investments and unfavorable mix. Chemical analysis fell $78 million, partly offset by electron microscopy (+$87 million).

**Life Sciences Solutions**

Revenue rose 8% to $10,374 million from $9,631 million, and segment income rose 8% to $3,768 million, leaving margin slightly lower at 36.3% from 36.4%. Bioproduction added $548 million on pharma and biotech demand, and genetic sciences added $82 million from Olink, acquired July 2024. Acquisitions, business mix and strategic investments cut margin, and productivity gains partly offset it.

**Specialty Diagnostics**

Revenue rose 4% to $4,676 million from $4,512 million, and segment income rose 8% to $1,256 million from $1,159 million. Margin widened 1.2 points to 26.9% from 25.7%, which the company attributes to strong productivity improvements. Clinical diagnostics (+$52 million), immunodiagnostics (+$48 million) and transplant diagnostics (+$37 million) drove reported growth.

**Laboratory Products and Biopharma Services**

Revenue rose 4% to $23,984 million from $23,157 million, and segment income rose 8% to $3,350 million from $3,090 million, lifting margin 0.7 points to 14.0%. Pharma services (+$457 million) and research and safety (+$422 million) led growth, while COVID-19 vaccine and therapy activity moderated. Exceptionally strong productivity more than offset unfavorable mix and strategic investments.

**Scope note:** The filing gives no market share figures and describes no strategic partnerships beyond the acquisitions cited above. It gives no product-level innovation revenue data.

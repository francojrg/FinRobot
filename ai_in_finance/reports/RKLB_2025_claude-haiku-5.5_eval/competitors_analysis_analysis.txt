# Rocket Lab Corporation (RKLB) Versus Firefly Aerospace Inc. (FLY), Redwire Corporation (RDW), and Intuitive Machines, Inc. (LUNR)

**Data caveats.** The resource does not state the currency or unit scale for revenue, gross profit, EBITDA, or FCF, so these are discussed as reported. Fiscal year ends may differ across the four companies, so the year labels are used as given and cannot be verified as calendar-aligned. Firefly Aerospace Inc. has data only from 2023 onward, and its 2023 revenue growth is unavailable because that year is its first in the series. EV/EBITDA is "n/m" for every company in every year, so valuation must be read through EV/Sales and P/B instead. Several FCF Conversion and ROIC figures are either not meaningful or internally inconsistent with the EBITDA figures, and those issues are flagged below. Where I cite FCF as a percentage of revenue, I computed it from the reported figures.

## Rocket Lab Corporation: Year-over-Year Trends

Revenue rose from 211 in 2022 to 245 in 2023 (15.9% growth), then accelerated sharply to 436 in 2024 (78.3% growth) before moderating to 602 in 2025 (38.0% growth). The 2024 step-up was the defining event in the series, and the business has kept compounding since, although growth has slowed from its 2024 pace.

Gross margin expanded in every year, from 9.0% in 2022 to 21.0%, 26.6%, and 34.4% in 2025. That is a gain of roughly 25 percentage points over three years and is the most consistent positive trend in the company's data. Gross profit grew from 19 to 207 over the same period.

EBITDA margin improved from -45.1% in 2022 to -53.8% in 2023 (a deterioration), then -29.7% in 2024 and -25.8% in 2025. Absolute EBITDA losses, however, widened from -95 to -155, so the margin improvement came from revenue outgrowing losses rather than from losses shrinking.

FCF was negative every year and became more negative in 2025, falling to -322 from -116 in 2024. Expressed against revenue, the 2025 burn equals about 53% of sales, compared with about 27% in 2024. This is the most significant deterioration in the company's trend data.

ROIC was negative in all four years, at -12.0%, -18.2%, -15.5%, and -9.3%. The 2025 value is the least negative in the series, which suggests the return profile is improving even though it remains below zero.

EV/Sales rose from 8.07 in 2022 to 11.11, 29.91, and 67.38 in 2025, an expansion that far outpaced revenue growth. P/B moved from 2.66 to 4.88, then 33.59, before easing to 23.88 in 2025.

## Competitor Comparison by Year

Its EBITDA margin of -45.1% was weaker than Intuitive Machines, Inc. (-5.3%) and better than Redwire Corporation (-74.2%). On ROIC, Rocket Lab Corporation's -12.0% lagged Intuitive Machines, Inc. (14.8%) but outperformed Redwire Corporation (-171.5%). Its revenue of 211 was the largest in the group, and its EV/Sales of 8.07 was far above Redwire Corporation (1.21) and Intuitive Machines, Inc. (2.10).

Its EBITDA margin of -53.8% was the second-weakest in the group, ahead only of Firefly Aerospace Inc. (-224.3%) and well behind Redwire Corporation (-2.6%) and Intuitive Machines, Inc. (-73.9%). Revenue of 245 remained the largest. Its ROIC of -18.2% lagged Firefly Aerospace Inc. (31.5%) and Intuitive Machines, Inc. (23.6%) and was also below Redwire Corporation (-37.4%). Its EV/Sales of 11.11 compared with Redwire Corporation at 1.07 and Intuitive Machines, Inc. at 1.17, a premium of roughly ten times.

**2024.** Rocket Lab Corporation led the group on gross margin at 26.6%, ahead of Redwire Corporation (14.6%), Intuitive Machines, Inc. (1.2%), and Firefly Aerospace Inc. (-18.7%). Its EBITDA margin of -29.7% was marginally better than Redwire Corporation (-30.0%) and far better than Intuitive Machines, Inc. (-151.3%) and Firefly Aerospace Inc. (-321.8%). Its revenue of 436 was the largest, and its 78.3% growth was the fastest of the group except Intuitive Machines, Inc., whose 186.6% growth came off a low base of 80 in 2023 and reflected a one-time jump in revenue. On ROIC, Rocket Lab Corporation's -15.5% trailed Firefly Aerospace Inc. (33.6%), Intuitive Machines, Inc. (25.6%), and Redwire Corporation (160.5%). Its EV/Sales of 29.91 compared with Redwire Corporation at 3.90 and Intuitive Machines, Inc. at 7.27.

**2025.** Rocket Lab Corporation's gross margin of 34.4% was the highest in the group, ahead of Firefly Aerospace Inc. (19.2%), Redwire Corporation (5.2%), and Intuitive Machines, Inc. (2.9%). Its EBITDA margin of -25.8% was the least negative, compared with Intuitive Machines, Inc. (-45.9%), Redwire Corporation (-53.4%), and Firefly Aerospace Inc. (-181.9%). Rocket Lab Corporation's EV/Sales of 67.38 was roughly 3.5 times Firefly Aerospace Inc.'s 19.26, about 7.9 times Intuitive Machines, Inc.'s 8.49, and about 15 times Redwire Corporation's 4.43.

## Metric-Specific Insights

**EBITDA Margin.** Rocket Lab Corporation's margin has been negative throughout, but its 2024 and 2025 readings place it at or near the top of the peer group. In 2025 it was the least negative of the four. Its profitability picture therefore looks better relative to peers than in absolute terms, since all four companies were loss-making at the EBITDA level. Redwire Corporation posted the best margin in 2023 at -2.6%, which was an outlier year for that company, and Intuitive Machines, Inc. was the best in 2022 at -5.3%. Rocket Lab Corporation's 2025 result of -25.8% represents steady improvement from its own 2023 low, but the absolute EBITDA loss of -155 is the largest in the group. Firefly Aerospace Inc.'s losses also grew, to -291 in 2025, so the scale of loss is not unique to Rocket Lab Corporation.

**EV/EBITDA.** This ratio is "n/m" for all four companies in every year, so it cannot be used to determine whether Rocket Lab Corporation is over or undervalued relative to peers. The most reliable valuation evidence available is EV/Sales, where Rocket Lab Corporation has traded at a steadily widening premium. Its multiple was about 8 times in 2022, roughly 11 in 2023, nearly 30 in 2024, and about 67 in 2025. Peers in 2025 traded between 4.43 and 19.26 times sales. Relative to every peer that had a reported multiple in each year, Rocket Lab Corporation has been the most expensive. Given that its EBITDA is negative, a premium on EV/Sales does not translate into a conventional earnings-based comparison, and the cause of the premium cannot be determined from these metrics alone.

**FCF Conversion.** Rocket Lab Corporation's FCF Conversion is "n/m" in all years, so the metric cannot be used for direct comparison. Comparing FCF to revenue instead, Rocket Lab Corporation's cash burn was about 27% of revenue in 2024 and about 53% in 2025. Intuitive Machines, Inc. was at a similar level in 2024 (about 30%) and better in 2025 (about 27%). Redwire Corporation burned about 9% of revenue in 2024 but about 60% in 2025, and Firefly Aerospace Inc. burned far more than its revenue in both years, at roughly 311% and 149%. On this basis Rocket Lab Corporation's cash efficiency was middling in 2024 and weaker than Intuitive Machines, Inc. in 2025. The only numeric FCF Conversion value in the group, Intuitive Machines, Inc.'s -1.22 in 2023, is computed against negative EBITDA and should not be interpreted as a meaningful efficiency signal.

**Gross Margin.** Rocket Lab Corporation's gross margin rose steadily from 9.0% to 34.4%, which reflects improving unit economics as the business scaled. The 2022 and 2023 readings lagged Redwire Corporation and, in 2023, Firefly Aerospace Inc., indicating that cost efficiency was weaker in early years. By 2024 and 2025 Rocket Lab Corporation led the group, while Redwire Corporation's margin fell from 23.8% in 2023 to 5.2% in 2025, and Intuitive Machines, Inc. stayed near break-even. Firefly Aerospace Inc.'s swing from 48.2% in 2023 to -18.7% in 2024 and back to 19.2% in 2025 shows how volatile margins can be for companies at an early stage of production.

**ROIC.** Rocket Lab Corporation's ROIC has been negative in every year, indicating that it has not yet generated returns above its capital base on a sustained basis. The improvement to -9.3% in 2025 is the best of its recent trend and is consistent with the rising gross margin and narrowing EBITDA margin. However, several peer ROIC figures are difficult to reconcile with their EBITDA figures. Firefly Aerospace Inc. shows positive ROIC of 31.5% and 33.6% in 2023 and 2024 despite negative EBITDA in both years. Redwire Corporation shows 160.5% in 2024 despite EBITDA of -91, and Intuitive Machines, Inc. shows positive ROIC in every year despite negative EBITDA in all four years. These figures probably reflect differences in how invested capital or return is defined, and they should not be treated as evidence that those peers generated superior returns. On the face of the numbers, Rocket Lab Corporation's ROIC is weaker than Intuitive Machines, Inc.'s in 2025, but that comparison depends on the same definitional questions.

**Revenue and Revenue Growth.** Rocket Lab Corporation's revenue grew from 211 to 602 over the four years, the largest absolute expansion in the group and the largest revenue base in each year. Its growth followed a pattern of modest expansion in 2023, a sharp acceleration in 2024, and continued high growth in 2025 at a slower rate. Its 2025 growth of 38.0% trails Firefly Aerospace Inc.'s 163.0%, which came from a very small base, but is far higher than Redwire Corporation's 10.3%. Intuitive Machines, Inc.'s revenue has been volatile, with a 186.6% jump in 2024 followed by a 9.2% decline in 2025, which suggests lumpy contract-driven revenue. Rocket Lab Corporation's revenue trajectory appears more consistent and more diversified in scale than any peer's, although the data does not show the sources of its revenue.

## Conclusion

Taken together, the metrics describe a company whose operating economics are improving but whose cash consumption is still heavy. Rocket Lab Corporation has scaled revenue to 602, the largest in the peer group, and has lifted gross margin to 34.4%, the highest among the four companies in 2025. EBITDA margin has improved from -53.8% in 2023 to -25.8% in 2025, and ROIC has moved toward zero. These trends are real and support the view that the business is maturing in its core unit economics.

The contrary evidence is substantial. Absolute EBITDA losses reached -155 in 2025, and FCF burn more than doubled to -322, which is roughly half of revenue. ROIC remains negative in every year. Because the company's EBITDA is negative, EV/EBITDA cannot be used, and the market's valuation is expressed only through EV/Sales of 67.38 and P/B of 23.88. Those multiples are many times the levels of peers such as Redwire Corporation at 4.43 and 1.28, and Firefly Aerospace Inc. at 19.26 and 2.99. The performance shown in these metrics supports a premium for growth and for the improving gross margin trajectory, but it does not, on its own, account for a multiple of this size. The current valuation therefore appears to rest substantially on expectations of future earnings and cash generation that are not yet visible in the reported figures. Whether that premium is justified depends on the company's ability to convert its revenue growth into positive EBITDA and free cash flow, which the data shows it has not yet achieved.

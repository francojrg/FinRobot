Total liabilities rose 44% to $180.0 billion at December 31, 2025, faster than total assets, which grew 32% to $595.3 billion. Total debt more than doubled to $59.3 billion from $22.6 billion, mainly because of $37.3 billion of net senior note proceeds in 2025. Debt-to-equity moved from 0.07 to 0.14 (illustrative, using $415.3 billion of equity), which remains low. Liquidity improved: the current ratio (illustrative) rose to 2.01 from 1.84, and cash and short-term securities reached $126.8 billion. Net PP&E grew 42% to $261.8 billion, reflecting $91.4 billion of capital expenditures, so the asset base is shifting toward infrastructure. Off-balance-sheet commitments are large: $52.7 billion of data center lease payments not yet commenced and $149.1 billion of purchase obligations. Operating cash flow of $164.7 billion still covers these needs. The view would be wrong if debt keeps growing faster than operating cash flow.

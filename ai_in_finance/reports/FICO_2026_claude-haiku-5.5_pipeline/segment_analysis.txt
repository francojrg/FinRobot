# Segment Analysis: FICO, Fiscal 2026 vs. Fiscal 2025

The company reports two segments, **Scores** and **Software**. Segment results do not include net income, so company-level net income is shown below for context. Total revenue rose 27% to $2,534.9 million, operating income rose 37% to $1,265.7 million, net income rose 28% to $835.0 million, and diluted EPS rose 37% to $36.32. Unallocated items moved the operating result away from segment totals. Unallocated corporate expenses rose $79.5 million to $261.0 million, and unallocated restructuring charges were $27.3 million, up from $10.9 million.

## Scores Segment

Scores revenue rose $483.6 million (41%) to $1,652.2 million, mainly from business-to-business mortgage origination scores on higher unit prices and volume. Segment operating income rose 45% to $1,489.9 million, and margin widened to 90% from 88% as segment expenses grew $20.0 million. In April 2026, FHFA approved FICO Score 10T for the conforming mortgage market. Market share is not disclosed.

## Software Segment

Software revenue rose $60.4 million (7%) to $882.7 million, led by on-premises and SaaS (+$68.0 million), partly offset by professional services (-$7.6 million). Operating income rose $1.9 million to $249.6 million, but margin fell to 28% from 30%, attributed to higher third-party data center hosting costs and fewer point-in-time license sales. Platform ARR: $452.6 million, 53% of total.

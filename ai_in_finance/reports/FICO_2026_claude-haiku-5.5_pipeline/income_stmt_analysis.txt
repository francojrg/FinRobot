**Revenue Growth:** FY2026 revenue rose 27% to $2,534.9M from $1,990.9M, led by Scores (up 41% to $1,652.2M). Software grew 7%.

**Costs and Margins:** Gross margin widened to 86.1% from 82.2% as cost of revenues fell 1% to $351.5M. Operating margin reached 50% (46% in FY2025), but SG&A rose 33% to $681.1M, including a $65.0M antitrust settlement accrual.

**Profitability:** Net margin was flat at 33%. Interest expense rose 67% to $223.3M on higher average debt, and the tax rate climbed to 24.1% from 18.8%.

**EPS:** Diluted EPS grew 37% to $36.32, ahead of the 28% rise in net income, helped by 6% fewer diluted shares.

**Watch Items:** Software segment margin fell to 28% from 30%, and non-platform ARR declined 18% year over year while platform ARR rose 72%.

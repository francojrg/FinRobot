**Software (fiscal 2026 vs. fiscal 2025)**

Software revenue rose 7% to $882.7 million, but segment operating income was essentially flat at $249.6 million, and the segment margin fell to 28% from 30%, while Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) reached $850.9 million at September 30, 2026, up 14% from a year earlier. The margin decline came mainly from higher third-party data center hosting costs and a decrease in higher-margin software recognized at a point in time, which fell $12.1 million (13%). Growth came from FICO Platform: its ARR was $452.6 million, 53% of software ARR and up 72% year over year, while non-platform ARR fell 18% to $398.3 million.

**Scores (fiscal 2026 vs. fiscal 2025)**

Scores revenue rose 41% to $1,652.2 million from $1,168.6 million, and segment operating income rose 45% to $1,489.9 million, lifting the segment margin to 90% from 88%. The increase came mainly from business-to-business scores, which rose $473.6 million on both a higher unit price and higher volume of mortgage origination scores, while business-to-consumer revenue added $10.0 million, driven by royalties on scores sold indirectly to consumers through credit reporting agencies.

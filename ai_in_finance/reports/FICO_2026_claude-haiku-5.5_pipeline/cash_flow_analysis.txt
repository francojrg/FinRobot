Fiscal 2026 operating cash flow rose to $1,072.1 million from $778.8 million in fiscal 2025, and free cash flow (operating cash flow less $37.7 million of capital expenditure) rose to $1,034.4 million from $739.4 million. The weaker part of the picture is financing. Share repurchases of $3,046.0 million were roughly three times free cash flow (illustrative ratio), and net debt issuance of $2,206.6 million funded the gap, lifting total debt to $5.3 billion from $3.1 billion. Interest paid more than doubled to $224.4 million from $103.6 million. Operating cash flow is also not fully recurring: the MD&A attributes $69.8 million of the increase to timing of receipts and payments, and $185.5 million of stock-based compensation is a non-cash add-back. Cash ended at $217.6 million, up $83.5 million. Liquidity covers the $337.5 million of term loan principal due within twelve months, but the view weakens if free cash flow stops covering interest and scheduled repayments while buybacks continue at this pace.

**Other Bets:** Revenues fell to $1.5 billion in 2025 from $1.6 billion in 2024, while the operating loss widened to $7.5 billion from $4.4 billion, an increase of $3.1 billion. The larger loss was primarily driven by higher employee compensation expenses, mainly from an increase in a valuation-based compensation charge related to Waymo, including a $2.1 billion charge recognized in the fourth quarter and primarily reflected in research and development expenses.

**Google Services:** Revenues rose 12% to $342.7 billion from $304.9 billion, and operating income increased $18.1 billion to $139.4 billion, which implies an operating margin of about 41%, up from about 40% in 2024 (illustrative, derived from the segment revenue and operating income figures). The increase in operating income was primarily driven by higher revenues, led by Google Search & other, which grew $26.4 billion to $224.5 billion on higher search query volume from mobile usage, advertiser spending, and ad format improvements, partially offset by higher legal and other matter expenses (including the $3.5 billion EC fine accrued in the third quarter of 2025), TAC, and content acquisition costs.

**Google Cloud:** Revenues increased 36% to $58.7 billion from $43.2 billion, and operating income more than doubled to $13.9 billion from $6.1 billion, which implies an operating margin of about 24%, up from about 14% in 2024 (illustrative, derived from the segment figures). The growth was primarily driven by Google Cloud Platform, largely from infrastructure and platform services, while higher usage costs for technical infrastructure and employee compensation expenses partially offset the gain.

**Alphabet-level activities:** The operating loss widened to $16.8 billion from $10.5 billion, reflecting costs that are not allocated to the three segments. The filing states that these costs primarily reflect expenses related to Alphabet's shared AI research and development.

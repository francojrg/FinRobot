**Segment Overview**

Alphabet reports three segments: Google Services, Google Cloud, and Other Bets. Total operating income rose 15% to $129.0 billion in 2025 from $112.4 billion in 2024.

**Google Services**

Google Services revenue rose $37.8 billion (12%) to $342.7 billion, and operating income rose $18.1 billion (15%) to $139.4 billion. Search & other added $26.4 billion on paid clicks (+6%) and cost-per-click (+7%). Offsets were legal charges, including a $3.5 billion EC fine, and TAC, which rose $5.0 billion, while the TAC rate eased to 20.3%.

**Google Cloud**

Google Cloud revenue rose $15.5 billion (36%) to $58.7 billion, and operating income rose $7.8 billion to $13.9 billion, a margin of 23.7% versus 14.1% (illustrative calculation). Higher usage costs for technical infrastructure and employee compensation partly offset revenue growth, which came mainly from Google Cloud Platform. Wiz ($32.0 billion, pending) is expected to close in 2026. Market share is not disclosed.

**Other Bets**

Other Bets revenue fell $111 million (7%) to $1.5 billion, while the operating loss widened $3.1 billion to $7.5 billion. The increase came mainly from employee compensation, including a $2.1 billion valuation-based charge for Waymo recognized in the fourth quarter. In February 2026, Waymo announced a $16.0 billion funding round, the significant majority of which Alphabet funded.

**Alphabet-level Activities (not a reportable segment)**

The operating loss grew $6.2 billion to $16.8 billion, primarily reflecting expenses for shared AI research and development that are not allocated to the three segments.

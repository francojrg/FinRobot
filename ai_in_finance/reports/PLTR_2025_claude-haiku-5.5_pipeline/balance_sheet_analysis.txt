Palantir's balance sheet at December 31, 2025 is liquid and nearly unlevered: assets of $8.90 billion, liabilities of $1.41 billion, and equity up to $7.39 billion from $5.00 billion. Current assets of $8.36 billion cover current liabilities of $1.18 billion about 7.1 times, up from 6.0 times in 2024. Capital lease obligations of $229 million equal about 3% of equity (5% in 2024), and the company reports no borrowings. Cash and short-term investments of $7.18 billion are 81% of assets, but cash itself fell to $1.42 billion as $5.75 billion moved into short-term investments. Receivables rose 81% to $1.04 billion while revenue grew 56%; illustratively, collection time lengthened from about 73 to 85 days. The view weakens if receivables keep outrunning revenue or $1.76 billion of cloud commitments strain cash.

**Segments identified:** The company reports two operating segments, Government and Commercial. Its chief operating decision maker is the CEO.

**Government**

Government revenue rose $832.7 million, or 53%, to $2.40 billion in 2025 from $1.57 billion in 2024, and reached 54% of total revenue. Of the increase, $774.0 million came from customers existing at December 31, 2024. The 10-K flags budget and timing uncertainty for large government customers as a risk to this growth. Segment profit is not disclosed.

**Commercial**

Commercial revenue rose $777.3 million, or 60%, to $2.07 billion in 2025 from $1.30 billion in 2024, and reached 46% of total revenue. Of the increase, $425.2 million came from existing commercial customers, which includes a $37.0 million revenue decline from Strategic Commercial Contracts. U.S. commercial revenue rose 109% to $1.5 billion. Commercial total remaining deal value was $6.8 billion, up 117%.

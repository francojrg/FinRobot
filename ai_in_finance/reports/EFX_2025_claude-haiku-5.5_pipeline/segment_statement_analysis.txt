## Workforce Solutions

Workforce Solutions revenue rose 6% to $2,582.3 million in 2025 from $2,433.8 million, and operating income rose 8% to $1,141.5 million, lifting margin to 44.2% from 43.3%. Employer Services fell 2% to $402.5 million from $411.9 million on lower unemployment claims, ACA and I-9 revenue. Verification Services grew 8% to $2,179.8 million on diversified and mortgage growth.

## U.S. Information Solutions (USIS)

USIS revenue rose 10% to $2,078.5 million from $1,893.0 million, and operating income rose 18% to $475.2 million, lifting margin to 22.9% from 21.4%. Mortgage growth came from product pricing, partly offset by lower mortgage credit inquiry volumes, and operating expenses rose on higher mortgage-related royalty costs. Financial Marketing Services grew 6% to $257.1 million.

## International

International revenue rose 4% to $1,413.7 million from $1,354.3 million, or 6% in local currency, after a $21.7 million foreign exchange drag. Operating income rose 1% to $182.5 million, but margin fell to 12.9% from 13.4% on higher people costs and amortization of capitalized internal-use software. Latin America grew 5% to $403.4 million; Europe grew 7% to $396.7 million.

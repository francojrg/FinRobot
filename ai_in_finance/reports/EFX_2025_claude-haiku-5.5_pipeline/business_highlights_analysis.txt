**Workforce Solutions:** Revenue rose 6% to $2,582.3 million in 2025 from $2,433.8 million in 2024, and operating margin widened to 44.2% from 43.3%, even though Employer Services revenue fell 2% to $402.5 million. Verification Services grew 8% to $2,179.8 million on diversified-market growth in government, talent solutions and consumer lending, plus mortgage growth, while Employer Services declines came from unemployment claims, ACA and I-9 revenue, partly offset by work opportunity tax credit and identity theft protection services.

**U.S. Information Solutions (USIS):** Revenue rose 10% to $2,078.5 million, operating income rose 18% to $475.2 million, and operating margin improved to 22.9% from 21.4%. Online Information Solutions grew 10% to $1,821.4 million, with mortgage revenue growth driven mainly by product pricing and partly offset by lower mortgage credit inquiry volumes, while Financial Marketing Services grew 6% to $257.1 million on credit marketing services, and the margin gain was partly offset by higher mortgage-related royalty costs.

**International:** Revenue rose 4% to $1,413.7 million (6% in local currency), but operating margin fell to 12.9% from 13.4%, and operating income grew only 1% to $182.5 million. Latin America (up 5% to $403.4 million, or 10% in local currency) and Europe (up 7% to $396.7 million) drove growth, Asia Pacific grew just 2% to $342.3 million as a decline in India offset growth in Australia, and Canada rose 2% to $271.3 million; the margin decline reflects higher people costs and higher amortization of capitalized internal-use software from prior technology transformation spending.

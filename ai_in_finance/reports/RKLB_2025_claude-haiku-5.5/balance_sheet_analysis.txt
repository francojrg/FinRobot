Rocket Lab's 2025 balance sheet shows much stronger liquidity and equity, alongside heavier investment. Current assets rose to $1,365.5 million against $334.5 million of current liabilities, a current ratio of about 4.1x, up from 2.0x in 2024, with cash and equivalents reaching $828.7 million. Total debt fell to $254.0 million from $468.4 million, cutting debt-to-equity to about 0.15x from 1.22x, as $1,119.5 million of ATM equity proceeds lifted stockholders' equity to $1,721.9 million. However, the accumulated deficit widened to $1,011.9 million, and asset turnover slipped to about 0.26x from 0.37x as goodwill and intangibles rose to $430.5 million following the GEOST acquisition. Financial flexibility is strong, but Neutron's delayed timeline keeps execution and capital-intensity risks central.

Rocket Lab's 2025 cash flow shows growing reliance on outside capital. Operating cash flow fell to -$165.5 million from -$48.9 million in 2024, as the $198.2 million net loss was only partly offset by $71.1 million of stock-based compensation and $43.9 million of depreciation and amortization. Investing outflows of $347.4 million included $156.3 million of capex (up from $67.1 million), $132.4 million for the GEOST acquisition, and $59.1 million of net securities purchases. Financing inflows of $1,071.3 million came mainly from $1,119.5 million of ATM equity proceeds, partly offset by $61.5 million of net debt repayment. Free cash flow was about -$321.8 million, versus -$116.0 million. Cash of $828.7 million plus $270.2 million of marketable securities gives near-term liquidity, but the cash burn, Neutron delays, and $155.7 million of convertible notes mean continued dependence on equity markets. The filing does not mention dividends and assumes a zero dividend yield.
